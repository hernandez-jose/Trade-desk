## Style Profile
- **Base:** Kyokushin Karate (training since age 10); Master of Sports in Submission Fighting; 2013 ADCC Ukraine Champion
- **Stance:** Orthodox; long, rangy striker with heavy inside leg kick and spinning techniques
- **Striking:** Good volume at range; likes to use reach advantage and oblique/front kick to manage distance; dangerous with flying knees and spinning strikes
- **Grappling:** Elite submission hunter — 16 career submission wins (6 RNCs, 3 guillotines, 2 kimuras, 2 Achilles locks, 1 armbar, 1 triangle, 1 keylock); can threaten from top and bottom positions
- **Durability:** Questionable chin — suffered back-to-back R1 KO losses in 2025 (Reyes, Guskov); striking defense at 52% is below-average for elite 205
- **Cardio:** Fights tend to end early (KO or sub); limited sample of late-round performances in UFC
- **IQ:** High pressure, relentless attacker; can be reckless under fire which contributes to KO losses

## Notes
- Suffered consecutive R1 KO losses to Reyes and Guskov in 2025; chin questions are very real at this stage of career
- Rebounded with R3 KO over Bukauskas at UFC 324 (Jan 2026) — stayed active and proved finishing ability
- Has 3-inch height advantage and 4-inch reach advantage over Whittaker — significant natural physical edge at LHW
- Always been a natural light heavyweight; fights at his best walking weight, no brutal cut
- Background in Kyokushin karate gives him unusual kicking and timing patterns; mixes in high-level sub grappling
- UFC rankings: #13 LHW as of mid-2025 following consecutive losses
- Notable wins include: Alexander Gustafsson (R1 KO, July 2022), Volkan Oezdemir (UD, Oct 2022), Ryan Spann (Sub R1, Mar 2023)
