# Mauricio Ruffy — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Freedom 250 | vs. Michael Chandler | June 14, 2026 | 155 lbs | 3 Rounds

> Analyst note: A 29-year-old, in-form, taller, longer, more accurate power striker facing a 40-year-old name on a three-fight skid and a ~14-month layoff. ## 1. Executive Summary
**Case FOR:** Ruffy is younger (29 vs 40), longer (75" reach vs 71"), taller (5'11" vs 5'8"), more accurate (58% vs 46%), and absorbs far less damage. He owns a ~92% finish rate and a recent statement TKO of Rafael Fiziev, plus a viral spinning-wheel-kick KO of King Green. Chandler is hittable (SApM 4.58), declining, rusty, and increasingly chooses to brawl — handing Ruffy the striking battle he dominates.
**Case AGAINST:** Ruffy's lone UFC loss exposed a real hole: Benoît Saint Denis took him down inside the first minute and finished him by Round 2. Chandler is a D1 All-American wrestler with the tools to replicate that blueprint and the power to end any exchange. If Chandler commits to takedowns, Ruffy's defensive grappling becomes the deciding variable.
**Most likely path:** Ruffy controls distance, beats Chandler up at range, and finishes a declining opponent by KO/TKO in Rounds 1–2.

## 3. Opponent Context
Chandler threatens Ruffy in exactly one dimension: wrestling. The Saint Denis loss is the template — early takedown, top control, finish. But Chandler has drifted away from wrestling toward brawling, and a striking match with a 40-year-old, hittable, rusty opponent is precisely the fight Ruffy wants. Chandler's power is the live danger — one clean overhand right keeps any 3-rounder competitive — but Ruffy's range, footwork, and low absorption are built to neutralize a shorter pressure brawler. ([ATS.io preview](https://ats.io/mma/mauricio-ruffy-vs-michael-chandler-prediction-preview-ufc-freedom-250-6-14-2026/519266/))

## 5. Grappling Analysis
### Offensive Wrestling
Minimal — he's a striker who doesn't shoot. Not part of his game plan here.
### Defensive Wrestling
The question mark. TD defense reads 85% on paper, but Saint Denis took him down inside the first minute, suggesting the number flatters him against a determined high-level wrestler.
### Scrambles & Submissions
Saint Denis finished him on the mat (Round 2 neck crank/RNC), indicating limited bottom-game defense and scramble ability under a dominant grappler. Zero offensive submission threat.
### Cage Wrestling
Unproven defensively against sustained cage pressure; the Saint Denis fight is the cautionary data point.

## 11. Key Paths to Victory
### Path 1: KO/TKO at range, R1–R2 (High)
Ruffy uses reach and footwork, lands clean (straights, kicks, spinning attacks), and finishes a hittable, declining Chandler early. The model's primary path.
### Path 2: Decision via range control (Medium)
Chandler survives but can't close distance or sustain wrestling; Ruffy outlands him over 15 minutes.
### Path 3: Avoid the grappling trap (Enabling condition)
If Chandler wrestles, Ruffy must defend early takedowns and win scrambles; doing so funnels back into Paths 1–2.

## 12. X-Factors
- **Chandler's power:** The one variable that can flip a winning fight — Ruffy must respect the overhand right.
- **Grappling exposure:** If Chandler shocks everyone and wrestles disciplined, Ruffy's bottom game is the soft spot.
- **Spinning-attack variance:** High-reward, slightly higher-risk offense; a missed spin against a wrestler can invite a takedown.
- **Stage/occasion:** White House card — Ruffy embraces the spotlight, momentum favors the rising star.

## 13. Risk Factors & Confidence Assessment
### Primary Risks to Ruffy Winning
(1) Chandler's one-punch KO power in any exchange; (2) Chandler committing to wrestling and exploiting Ruffy's defensive-grappling hole (the Saint Denis blueprint). Both are real but require Chandler to fight against his recent tendencies and/or land clean early.
### Confidence Level
Model estimate for Ruffy: **~84% to win** (capped near the model's 88% ceiling because Chandler's power + wrestling pedigree are genuine variance). Most likely outcome is **Ruffy KO/TKO in R1–R2**. Implied probability of the favorite is ~84% (-520) to ~89% (-800), so the flat moneyline offers **no edge** at these prices. The value sits in props: Ruffy by KO/TKO, Under 1.5 rounds, or the Ruffy + Under SGP near even money — these capture the likeliest scenario at a fair-to-slightly-positive price.
