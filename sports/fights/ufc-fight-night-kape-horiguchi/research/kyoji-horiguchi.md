# Kyoji Horiguchi — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Fight Night: Kape vs. Horiguchi (UFC Vegas 119) | vs. Manel Kape | June 20, 2026 | 125 lbs | 5 Rounds (Main Event)

> Analyst note: Horiguchi is the more complete, higher-IQ fighter and already owns a finish over Kape (arm-triangle, 2017). But he's 35, only two fights into a UFC return after ~9 years away, has been KO'd twice, and the market has moved off him from a heavy-favorite opener to a modest underdog. The case for him is tactical (speed + grappling + experience banks rounds); the case against is age, chin, and Kape's ascending power.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR:** Horiguchi (35) is arguably the most complete fighter in the division's history — karate speed, real wrestling (2.0 TD avg), and finishing grappling (RNC of Ulanbekov R3). He beat Kape once (arm-triangle, RIZIN 2017) and just outpointed Amir Albazi over three rounds (UD). Elite fight IQ and championship pedigree (RIZIN x2, Bellator BW champ). **AGAINST:** He's 35 with two KO losses on his record (Asakura, Pettis), only recently back in the UFC after ~9 years elsewhere, and faces a younger, heavier-handed man at his physical peak over 5 rounds. **Most likely Horiguchi path:** out-speed and grapple-mix to a decision, or catch a submission in a scramble.

## 3. Opponent Context
Kape is exactly the kind of explosive, never-been-KO'd power striker who tests Horiguchi's biggest liability — his chin. Kape's 77% TDD means Horiguchi can't count on easy takedowns; he'll need to set them up off strikes and win scrambles. The reach edge belongs to Kape. Horiguchi must avoid a pure firefight and instead fragment the fight with level changes, clinch, and angles.
