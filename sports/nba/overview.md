# NBA Betting — 2026 Playoffs Overview

## Current Status (April 2026)
The NBA Playoffs are underway — **First Round** in progress.  
Playoff basketball is the sharpest NBA market. Edges are smaller than in the regular season, but they exist.

## Playoff-Specific Betting Principles

### Home Court Matters More
Regular season home-field advantage: ~3–3.5 points  
Playoff home-field advantage: **4–5 points** (crowd pressure amplified, fatigue factor)

Historical ATS data:
- Home teams in Playoff Round 1: Cover ~53% at posted lines
- Home teams after losing Game 1: Cover ~57% (oddsmakers don't adjust fully for bounce-back)
- Road teams in elimination games: Cover ~52% (backs against the wall motivation)

### Series Price vs. Game-by-Game
- **Series prices** (who wins the series) are less efficient than individual game lines
- Series dogs with strong home courts are undervalued
- If a 6-seed has home court and a strong defense, the series price is often 15–20% off true probability

### Totals in the Playoffs
Playoff totals are generally 3–5 points lower than regular-season equivalents. Why:
- Slower pace (coaches prioritize execution over pace)
- More half-court sets, fewer transition points
- Defensive intensity increases significantly
- Stars get double-teamed more

**Lean**: Under is a slight favorite in playoff games early in a series (unfamiliarity with defensive schemes). By Games 5–7, the under edge diminishes as teams adjust.

## Key NBA Playoff Angles

### Back-to-Back Series Games (2-2-1-1-1 Format)
Games 3 and 4 are in the lower seed's city:
- Game 3 after two road games for the favorite: Road fatigue, lean home dog
- Game 4 is the "must-win" game for the home team if down 0-3: Backs-against-wall spot

### Rest Advantage
Rest days between games matter:
- Team with 2 extra rest days covers the spread ~57% of the time (historical)
- In a 7-game series, the team with more total rest days wins the series ~54% of the time

### Star Player Performance Prediction
- Stars who underperformed in the regular season vs. a specific matchup tend to regress to their mean in the playoffs
- Defensive assignments are locked in — if a star was held below average all year by this team, that's more likely to continue in the playoffs

## Regular Season Betting Framework (For Next Season Reference)

### Schedule and Rest Analysis
| Situation | ATS Lean |
|-----------|----------|
| Team on 4th game in 5 nights | Fade on the road |
| Back-to-back road second game | Lean home team |
| Home team coming off 3-day rest vs. back-to-back visitor | Strong home lean |
| Star resting on second night of b2b | Public still bets the team name; fade |

### Pace and Total Strategy
- High-pace teams (top 10 in possessions): Push totals up by 4–5 points from the book average
- Low-pace defensive teams (bottom 10): Push totals down by 4–5 points
- When a high-pace team plays a low-pace team, lean to the under (defensive team controls tempo)

### Key Numbers in NBA
Unlike NFL, the NBA doesn't have dominant key numbers, but:
- **4, 5, 6** are the most common margins
- Getting +4.5 instead of +4 or +5.5 instead of +5 is meaningful
- Half-point purchases in NBA totals are rarely worth the juice unless on an exact key number
