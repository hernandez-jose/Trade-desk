## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with Mané and Ismaïla Sarr as wide threats

**How they attack:**
Senegal attacks through pace and directness. Mané drifts inside and takes on defenders; Ismaïla Sarr provides pure pace on the right. Pape Matar Sarr's late runs from midfield are a secondary source of goals. Physical play and direct balls to Mané are key.

**How they defend:**
Mid-block 4-5-1 or 4-4-2. Solid African defensive discipline. Gana Gueye provides the defensive anchor. Vulnerable to technical combinations from elite European midfielders as shown vs France.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Direct — quick transitions  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium — aerial players  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
