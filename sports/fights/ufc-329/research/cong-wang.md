## Style Notes for This Matchup
- Wang's SLpM of 6.22 against Cortez's SApM of 3.01 suggests Wang will heavily outlanding Cortez in striking volume at far greater efficiency
- Wang's striking accuracy of 56% is elite for the division; against Cortez's 57% defense, clean shots will land at a high rate
- 100% takedown defense means Cortez cannot rely on the wrestling game plan that has worked vs. lower-level opponents — Wang will stuff every shot
- Wang's +4.03 striking differential (SLpM minus SApM) is dominant; Cortez's differential is +0.85 — Wang is vastly more efficient in both directions
- The only known weakness for Wang is submission vulnerability when hurt on the feet — Cortez has 47% takedown accuracy but 0 submissions on her UFC record; she cannot exploit this window
- Wushu Sanda + amateur boxing pedigree means Wang enters this fight with the most elite striking background Cortez has ever faced at 125 lbs
