## Style Notes for This Matchup
- **Scramble threat:** Royval's primary path to victory is dragging Kavanagh into scrambles — dirty boxing, clinch work, and takedown transitions to submission attempts
- **Submission setup:** If Royval gets Kavanagh to the mat, the submission danger is elite-level. Kavanagh's 1 submission win and purple belt BJJ may be insufficient against a black belt instructor
- **Striking exchange risk:** Royval absorbs 4.25 sig. strikes/min career — above Kavanagh's 4.29 output rate. Royval will be outgunned standing if the fight stays on the feet in clean exchanges
- **Southpaw vs. Orthodox:** Royval's southpaw stance creates left-hand power shot threats and angle advantages in the pocket; outside leg kick matchups favor Royval's lead left leg vs. Kavanagh's lead right leg
- **Grappling entry vs. TDD:** Royval's TD average (0.66/15 min) is low for a grappler — he typically seeks grappling through scrambles rather than clean double-leg shots. Kavanagh's 88.9% TDD could wall off clean shot attempts but may not prevent clinch scrambles
- **Chin concern:** Two KO/TKO losses; Kape landed a single right hand to finish. Kavanagh has KO power (4 stoppages career) and clean boxing — Royval's chin is a legitimate liability
