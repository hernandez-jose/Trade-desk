## Fighting Style

**Switch-stance counter-striker with a wrestling base.**

Raposo darts in and out, uses lateral movement, pot-shots and counters, and mixes in surprise takedowns set up off that movement. He was a state-champion wrestler, undefeated amateur kickboxer, and trained BJJ — a well-rounded regional standout. The knock is output: his counter-heavy approach leaves him behind on volume, and his three UFC fights all went the distance (1-2, both losses split).

### Grappling
- Wrestling base; surprise/reactive takedowns off movement rather than chain-wrestling pressure.
- Has a submission loss on the record — sub defense in scrambles is a question vs. an elite grappler.

### Striking
- Counter/pot-shot, lateral movement; reportedly weak leg- and body-kick defense.

### Cardio
- All UFC fights reached R3; competitive but volume sags.
