## 2025–26 Season Overview

The Magic finished 45–37 to earn the 8th seed in the East — a solid but unspectacular regular season. Their identity is built around two young stars (Banchero and Wagner) still developing chemistry and a defense-first system. They are not expected to go deep in the playoffs but have already exceeded expectations by beating the 1-seed on the road in Game 1.

**Offensive identity**: Banchero as the hub — power forward who can pass, score from three levels, and create for others. Wagner provides secondary creation. Bane spaces the floor and hits threes at a high clip.

**Defensive identity**: Team defense, switching schemes, protecting the paint. Banchero's 2 blocks and 3 steals in Game 3 show his defensive versatility as well.

**Home court**: The Kia Center has been a fortress. **26–16 home record** during the regular season. In these playoffs, Orlando has won every home game by a wide margin.
