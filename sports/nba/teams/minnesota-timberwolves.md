## 2025–26 Season Overview

Minnesota arrived in the playoffs as a 6-seed but has played like the better team against the 3-seed Denver. Their elite defensive identity — keyed by Gobert's rim protection and McDaniels' perimeter disruption — has neutralized Jokic's gravity and disrupted Denver's half-court sets.

The massive story of this series: **Anthony Edwards suffered a bone bruise/hyperextension in his left knee** that has him sidelined indefinitely. With DiVincenzo also lost to a torn Achilles, Minnesota has been operating short-handed. Dosunmu's 43-point explosion in Game 4 kept the dream alive and gave the Wolves a 3-1 series lead.

**Offensive identity**: Without Edwards, Minnesota relies on Dosunmu's scoring burst, Randle's interior play, and Gobert as an anchor. They win games by defending and grinding.

**Defensive identity**: Elite system. They force turnovers, protect the rim, and slow Jokic to manageable production. Best defensive unit in the West.
