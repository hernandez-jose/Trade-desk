## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-4-2 compact with counter-attack intent

**How they attack:**
New Zealand is direct and physical. Their primary threat is Elijah Just in wide positions who scored either side of halftime vs. Iran. They like to play on transitions and set pieces. Not a possession team — get the ball forward quickly to physical attackers.

**How they defend:**
Deep defensive block — usually a low 4-4-2. Disciplined and physical. They fouled Iran aggressively in MD1 to disrupt rhythm. Can concede from sustained pressure.
