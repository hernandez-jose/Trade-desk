## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-2-3-1 with Salah operating as a free-roaming #10/RW

**How they attack:**
Egypt's attack is largely Salah-dependent. He drops deep to link play, runs in behind, and takes on defenders one-on-one. Marmoush adds a second threat with his off-ball runs. Ashour gives them energy and goals from midfield. Set pieces are a source via Salah's delivery.

**How they defend:**
Egypt organizes in a 4-4-2 mid-block. They're disciplined and compact. The key risk is transitional speed — if the block is bypassed, the center backs can be exposed by pace.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Balanced — Salah dictates  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — Salah delivery + physical attackers  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
