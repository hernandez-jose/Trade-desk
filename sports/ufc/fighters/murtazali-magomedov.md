## Fighting Style

**Well-rounded Dagestani-style finisher — grappling-heavy with real KO power.**

Magomedov is an undefeated 10-0 prospect whose entire record is finishes, split evenly between knockouts and submissions. Described as a Dagestani grappler-type, he blends top-pressure wrestling and submission hunting with enough hands to end fights standing (R1 TKO to win his DWCS contract). The profile is a classic prospect-with-no-tape problem: aggressive, finish-or-bust, but unproven in the UFC, against ranked-level striking, or beyond the first round.

### Grappling
- 5 submission wins — genuine sub threat off takedowns and in scrambles.
- Dagestani-style chain wrestling and top control is the expected path vs. a non-wrestler.

### Striking
- 5 KO/TKO wins, including a fast R1 finish on DWCS — has pop, not just a grappler.
- Untested defensively vs. an elite kickboxer's volume and power.

### Cardio
- Unknown past R1 — has never needed deep cardio (0 decisions in 10 fights). 3-round stamina vs. UFC pace is an open question.
