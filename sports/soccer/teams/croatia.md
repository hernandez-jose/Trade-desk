## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 / 4-2-3-1 with Modrić as the deep orchestrator

**How they attack:**
Croatia's attack is built on Modrić's vision and technical quality. He recycles possession and picks passes from deep. Kramarić is a genuine goal threat in the box. Perišić provides wide running. Croatia are dangerous from set pieces — Kramarić aerial ability.

**How they defend:**
Croatia defend in a mid-block 4-5-1. Discipline is their hallmark under Dalić. The back four is experienced and organized. Main vulnerability: conceded 4 vs England — pace and direct play can expose them.

**Pressing Intensity:** Low-Medium  
**Possession Style:** Controlled — Modrić-led recycling  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium-High — Kramarić aerial + Modrić delivery  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
