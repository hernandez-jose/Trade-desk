# Betting Fundamentals

## The Three Core Bet Types

### 1. Moneyline (ML)
Bet on which team wins outright. No spread.

- **Favorite** is listed with a **minus** sign: `-150` means risk $150 to win $100
- **Underdog** is listed with a **plus** sign: `+130` means risk $100 to win $130
- Best for high-confidence wins where the spread feels risky

### 2. Point Spread (ATS — Against the Spread)
The favored team must win by more than the spread; the underdog must lose by less (or win outright).

```
Example: Chiefs -6.5 vs. Raiders +6.5
- Chiefs cover if they win by 7+
- Raiders cover if they lose by 6 or fewer, or win
```

Standard juice is **-110** on both sides — you pay 10¢ on the dollar for the book's margin.

### 3. Totals (Over/Under)
Bet on the combined score going over or under the posted number.

- Weather, injuries to offensive players, and pace of play are the main edges
- Typically priced at -110 each side
- Can be combined with spread (e.g., first-half totals)

## Converting Odds to Probability

### American to Implied Probability

**Negative odds (favorites):**
```
Implied % = |odds| / (|odds| + 100) × 100
Example: -150 → 150 / 250 × 100 = 60.0%
```

**Positive odds (underdogs):**
```
Implied % = 100 / (odds + 100) × 100
Example: +130 → 100 / 230 × 100 = 43.5%
```

### The Vig (Juice)
Both sides of a -110 line add to 104.8% (not 100%). The extra 4.8% is the book's margin.  
You must win **52.4%** of -110 bets just to break even.

## Key Betting Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| ATS | Against the Spread |
| CLV | Closing Line Value — did you beat the final line? |
| Steam | Sudden sharp money causing rapid line movement |
| Reverse Line Movement | Line moves opposite to public betting % (sharp signal) |
| Fade | Bet against a team or the public consensus |
| Juice / Vig | The book's commission built into the odds |
| Limit | Maximum allowed bet on a specific game |
| Action | A bet that counts regardless of postponement |
| No Action | Bet refunded (common in baseball when a starter doesn't pitch) |
| Correlated Parlay | Parlay where legs are statistically linked (books often ban these) |
| PK / Pick'em | Spread is zero — just pick the winner at -110 |
| Hook | The half-point in a spread (e.g., -3.5 has a "hook") |

## Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Betting too many games** — sharp bettors average 2–3 plays per day max
2. **Ignoring juice** — a -140 moneyline needs to win 58.3% to be profitable
3. **Chasing losses** — doubling down after a loss is the fastest path to going broke
4. **Betting with your heart** — your favorite team is the worst team to bet on
5. **No line shopping** — a half-point difference in spread wins or loses the bet
6. **Ignoring closing line value** — if you bet -3 and it closes -5, you won the line even if you lose the game

## The House Edge at -110

Over 1,000 bets at -110, to break even you need 524 wins (52.4%).  
A 55% win rate at -110 yields approximately +4.5 units per 100 bets — a respectable edge.  
A 58% win rate at -110 yields approximately +10 units per 100 bets — elite sharp territory.
