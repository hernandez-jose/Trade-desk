## Fighting Style

**Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt who fights like a brawler — finisher off both the feet and the mat.**

Lopes (2nd-degree BJJ black belt) blends genuine knockout power with elite submission grappling, giving him a finishing threat from nearly any position. He pushes a high-pressure, high-output pace and is comfortable in chaos, but he absorbs a lot of strikes (4.76 SApM, 46% defense) and can be tagged on the way in. His ceiling is high enough that he extended Volkanovski twice over five rounds.

### Grappling
- 2nd-degree BJJ black belt; 12 of 27 wins by submission. Armbar/back-take threat in any scramble.
- Low offensive takedown volume (0.35/15) — his grappling usually triggers off scrambles, not shots.

### Striking
- Real power (11 KO/TKO wins, including 1st-round finishes of Sabatini and Yusuff).
- Defensively porous: high absorption rate, swings with intent and can be countered.

### Cardio
- Carried solid output across two five-round championship fights vs. Volkanovski — proven 25-minute gas tank.
