*Weight: 15%*

**McGregor:**
- **Avg fight time:** Career average tilts toward R1–R2 finishes (14 first-round finishes). McGregor has shown late-round fatigue in longer fights (Nate Diaz fights, Khabib fight). (Source: ufcstats.com, Sherdog.com)

- **Avg fight time:** Holloway frequently goes the championship distance — his career has many 5-round fights, all at high volume.
- **Knockdown history:** Has been rocked but very rarely dropped. Topuria KO (R3, UFC 308, Oct 2024) was his first knockout loss in career. (Source: Sherdog.com, ESPN MMA)
- **Stoppage losses:** 1 KO/TKO loss (Topuria), 1 submission loss (Khabib). 8 career losses but only 2 stoppages — durable fighter.
- **Age:** 34 at fight — still within competitive prime.
- **Recent activity:** Fought in March 2026 — active and conditioned entering July 2026.

**Scoring rationale:**
- This is the most lopsided domain. Holloway's cardio advantage over a 37-year-old coming off 5 years out of the cage is enormous.
- McGregor's late-round cardio has always been questionable, and a 5-year layoff makes it worse.
- **McGregor Cardio & Durability Score: 4.0 / 10** (historically fades, 5-year layoff, age 37, chin tested and failed vs. Poirier)
- **Holloway Cardio & Durability Score: 9.2 / 10** (all-time UFC cardio reputation, last-second KO at UFC 300, only 2 career stoppages, age 34, active)

## Key Analytical Findings

### What the numbers say McGregor does well:
- **Elite finishing power:** 86% KO/TKO win rate and 14 first-round finishes — the southpaw left hand is a legitimate equalizer against any opponent on any given night (Source: ufcstats.com, Sherdog.com)
- **Reach and angle creation:** 5-inch reach advantage (74" vs. 69") combined with southpaw stance creates structural difficulty for orthodox opponents trying to manage distance (Source: UFC.com, Wikipedia)
- **Pressure and timing:** When McGregor is fresh (R1–R2), his counter-timing and distance management have historically been elite — he makes opponents reach for him and punishes overextension

### What the numbers say McGregor is vulnerable to:
- **Volume accumulation:** McGregor's 51% striking defense and 5.25 SApM means he is getting hit at a high rate; against Holloway's 7.17 SLpM, the damage compounds rapidly past Round 2 (Source: ufcstats.com)
- **Late-round deterioration:** The historical record shows McGregor fading significantly in rounds 3–5. Against a fighter whose defining trait is increasing output in championship rounds, this is catastrophic (Source: Sherdog.com, ESPN MMA)
- **5-year ring rust:** No competitive action since July 2021 entering a 5-round welterweight main event is unprecedented. Reflexes, timing, and championship conditioning all degrade meaningfully with this length of inactivity (Source: UFC.com, CBS Sports analysis)

### What the numbers say Holloway does well:
- **Volume and accumulation:** 7.17 SLpM is elite-tier output — the highest sustained strike rate among notable UFC champions. He holds the all-time record for significant strikes landed (3,655+), no one else within 1,000 (Source: UFC.com, ufcstats.com)
- **Championship-round cardio:** Holloway is historically one of the best-conditioned fighters in UFC history, routinely increasing output in R4–R5. The last-second KO at UFC 300 (R5, 4:59 vs. Gaethje) is the clearest demonstration (Source: ESPN MMA, Sherdog.com)
- **Takedown defense:** 84% TD defense neutralizes any clinch-and-dirty-box escape strategy McGregor might use to avoid Holloway's output (Source: ufcstats.com)

### What the numbers say Holloway is vulnerable to:
- **Elite KO power:** Holloway's first-ever KO loss (Topuria, UFC 308) showed that against elite punchers with precise power, his chin is not untouchable. McGregor's 86% KO rate against 22 opponents presents genuine danger in the first two rounds (Source: Sherdog.com)
- **Southpaw angle problems:** Holloway has struggled with southpaw fighters historically — the cross-angle of a southpaw left hand is the exact type of strike that bypassed his defense vs. Topuria; McGregor is equally dangerous from the same angle (Source: fight analysis, Low Kick MMA)
- **Shorter reach (69"):** Against McGregor's 74" reach and jab, Holloway will have to eat shots getting inside range in the opening rounds. This reach gap is the largest structural disadvantage he faces in this matchup (Source: UFC.com, Wikipedia)
