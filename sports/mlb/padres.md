## Petco Park — Pitcher's Paradise

Petco Park is consistently one of the most pitcher-friendly venues in MLB. Understanding this is essential for every Padres home bet.

| Metric                        | Petco Effect                              |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Run environment vs. neutral  | −1.0 to −1.5 runs per game                |
| HR factor                    | Suppressed — deep power alleys, marine air |
| Total adjustment              | Move total down 1–1.5 from neutral        |
| Weather impact               | Minimal — San Diego temps stay 60–75°F    |

**Rule**: Never bet the over at Petco Park unless you're getting significant value OR both teams are running hot offenses against weak pitching. The default is UNDER.

## Betting Angles

### Home Moneyline
- Padres are a **legitimate play as home favorites** when starting a quality arm at Petco
- **Lean**: Back Padres at home up to -145 with a quality starter (park amplifies pitching)
- **Avoid**: Padres as heavy home chalk (-165+) — juice kills value; park does the work, not the price

### Run Line
- **Padres -1.5 at Petco**: Value if starter is an ace and opposing offense is below average
- **Padres +1.5**: Low value — Petco already keeps games close; run-line odds are inefficient here

### Totals
- **Under is the default lean at Petco Park** — this is one of the strongest repeatable edges in MLB
- Only deviate to over if: both starters have ERA > 5.00 AND both bullpens are gassed
- Late-season totals at Petco often set too high due to recency bias from road games

### Road Games
- Padres road performance is unpredictable — monitor travel schedule
- Petco-conditioned pitchers can struggle in extreme hitter's parks (Coors, Great American)
- **April road edge**: Padres traveling east early in the year = jet lag factor; approach with caution
