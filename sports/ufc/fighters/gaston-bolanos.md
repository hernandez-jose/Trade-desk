## Fighting Style

**Creative Muay Thai striker / kickboxer with grappling vulnerabilities.**

Bolaños is a dynamic, highlight-oriented striker out of a Muay Thai and kickboxing base — known for kicks, elbows, and a signature spinning elbow that produced KOs in Bellator. He wants the fight standing at range and in the clinch. Both UFC defeats, however, came when opponents took him down or pressured into ground control, exposing his takedown defense and ground game as the path to beat him.

### Grappling
- Both UFC losses came via ground attack (TKO from top, then RNC). Takedown defense and scramble are the clear holes.

### Striking
- Genuine plus skill: varied Muay Thai arsenal, spinning attacks, clinch elbows, power.

### Cardio
- — (3-round fighter; no clear gas-tank red flags noted, but limited UFC distance sample.)
