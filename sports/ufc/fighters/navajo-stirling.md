## Fighting Style

**Rangy, composed City Kickboxing striker — volume kickboxer with championship-rounds discipline**

Stirling is a tall (6'4"), long (79" reach) light heavyweight who fights behind a jab and kicks, manages distance, and stays composed across three rounds. Built in the City Kickboxing system (Bareman), he applies steady pressure and out-volumes opponents (6.25 SLpM) while absorbing little (2.67 SApM). Strong takedown defense (82%) keeps the fight standing where he wants it. He has not been tested by elite competition or in deep adversity yet — the question marks are his chin under heavy power and his grappling offense, not his striking.

### Grappling
- Low TD output (0.98) — does not look to wrestle; uses grappling defensively.
- Excellent takedown defense (82%) keeps fights standing.
- Submission/scramble offense unproven at UFC level.

### Striking
- High volume (6.25 SLpM), efficient (52%), uses length and kicks.
- Kickboxing pedigree (WKBF world title, King in the Ring champ).

### Cardio
- Three UFC decision wins show he holds output across 15 minutes. Composed late.
