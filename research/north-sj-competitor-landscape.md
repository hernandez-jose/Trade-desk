# North San Jose — Competitor Landscape
*Researched May 2026. Use this as your secret shopper baseline before signing any lease.*

All restaurants below are within or immediately adjacent to the North San Jose corridor (ZIP codes 95134, 95131, and the Santa Clara border along Rivermark/Tasman).

---

## Primary Corridors

| Corridor | Character | Key Anchors |
|---|---|---|
| **Zanker Road (95134)** | Deep tech campus zone; Cisco, Intel, Broadcom in reach | Curry Up Now, various food trucks |
| **North First Street (95131)** | Mixed commercial; some residential; BART adjacent | Korean House, Dan Izakaya, Danbi Korean, Eight Forty |
| **Rivermark Plaza (Santa Clara / N. SJ border)** | Upscale strip mall; near Levi's Stadium | Rise Woodfire, national chains |
| **Trimble Road / Great America Pkwy** | Corporate hotel cluster; convention corridor | Hotel restaurants, limited options |
| **Crescent Village Circle** | Planned residential community; captive residents + workers | K On the Go (coffee), minimal dining |

### 1. Curry Up Now
**Address:** 3250 Zanker Rd, Ste 30, San Jose, CA 95134
**Cuisine:** Indian street food (fusion)
**Format:** Fast casual, full bar
**Price range:** $$
**Hours:** Mon–Thu 11:30am–10pm | Fri–Sat 11:30am–11pm | Sun 11:30am–9pm
**Yelp reviews:** 1,019 reviews (as of 2026)
**Platforms:** DoorDash, Uber Eats, EZCater (corporate catering)
**Reservations:** OpenTable

**Signature dishes:**
- Tikka Masala Burrito (most ordered)
- Kathi Rolls
- Lamb Bowl
- Naughty Naan
- Deconstructed Samosa
- Bhel Puri
- Thali Platters

**What customers say they love:**
- Fun, colorful atmosphere
- Unique Indian-fusion menu that appeals to non-Indian diners
- Full bar (strong cocktails)
- Corporate catering program; serves Cisco, Intel, Broadcom

**What customers complain about:**
- Portions on the small side for the price
- Menu is confusing for first-timers (too many options, unclear descriptions)
- Inconsistent food quality reported across visits
- Wait times can spike during tech campus lunch rush

**Competitive assessment:**
Curry Up Now is the dominant Indian food option in the Zanker corridor. High volume, brand recognition, and an active catering arm make them formidable. However, the portion/price perception gap and menu clarity issues are real vulnerabilities. A simpler, more generous Indian concept or a complementary non-Indian healthy fast casual could take significant share.

### 2. Rise Woodfire Pizza & Rotisserie
**Address:** 3905 Rivermark Plz, Santa Clara, CA 95054 *(Rivermark Plaza, North SJ border)*
**Cuisine:** Wood-fired pizza, rotisserie, seasonal dishes
**Format:** Full-service, full bar
**Price range:** $$$
**Hours:** Mon–Thu 11am–9pm | Fri–Sat 11am–10pm | Sun 11am–9pm
**Seating:** 300 seats
**Yelp reviews:** 299 reviews, 717 photos
**Reservations:** OpenTable
**Ownership:** Local/family-owned (Omid & Susan)
**Other locations:** San Mateo

**Signature dishes:**
- Wood-fired pizzas (unique house dough)
- Rotisserie plates
- Seasonal dishes
- Housemade pies
- Signature cocktails

**What customers say they love:**
- Surprisingly high quality for the concept
- Fast service; food comes out quickly
- Great family-friendly environment
- Proximity to Levi's Stadium (strong event dining)

**Competitive assessment:**
Rise is the closest thing to a polished full-service restaurant in the Rivermark/North SJ zone. Its 300-seat scale and full bar set a high bar for ambiance. However, wood-fired pizza + rotisserie is a narrow concept — there's no healthy bowl, no Asian influence, and the price point ($$+) means it's a dinner/occasion restaurant, not a lunch workhorse. Does not directly compete with a fast casual lunch concept.

### 4. Gen Korean BBQ

**Cuisine:** Korean BBQ (all-you-can-eat)

**Price range:** $$$
**Hours:** Daily 10:30am–11pm

### 5. BBQ Town (inside H Mart)
**Location:** Inside H Mart, North San Jose area
**Cuisine:** Korean / Japanese fusion
**Format:** Food court-style within Korean supermarket
**Price range:** $

**Signature items:**
- Pork rice bowls
- Galbi
- Ramen and tofu soup
- Udon noodles

**Competitive assessment:**
BBQ Town is the budget option for Korean/Japanese food in North SJ. The H Mart setting means it captures grocery shoppers and price-sensitive customers. Average check is low; quality is utilitarian. ## On-Campus Competition (Important Factor)

**Cisco Campus Cafeterias** (run by Bon Appétit Management)
- Multiple buildings; Building 17 at 3650 Cisco Way; Building J at 255 W Tasman Ave
- Estimated meals: $5–$7 (heavily subsidized)
- Offerings: breakfast, lunch, dinner rotations; hot stations, salad bars, international cuisines
- Yelp: 16 reviews (Building 17); 59 photos

**Strategic implication:** Any restaurant within 1 mile of Cisco must accept that a large portion of Cisco employees will eat on-campus due to subsidized pricing. Your target within the Cisco corridor is:
- The non-Cisco employees (Intel, Broadcom, Samsung, PayPal, startups don't have Cisco's subsidized cafeteria)
- Cisco employees who want to *leave* campus for lunch (they do — daily)
- Corporate catering for meetings and team events that require outside food

## The White Space in One Sentence

North San Jose in 2026 has **no high-quality, health-forward, lunch-dominant fast casual** with strong delivery + corporate catering capabilities that serves the 25–44 professional demographic. The category is wide open.
