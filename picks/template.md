```
## NBA | April 27, 2026 | Celtics vs. Knicks

**Pick**: Knicks +5.5 (-110)
**Bet Size**: 2 units
**Platform**: Klashi

### Analysis
**Spread/Line**: Opened Celtics -6 → moved to -5.5
**Public %**: 72% on Celtics — reverse line movement; line moved toward Knicks
**Injuries**: Celtics: Tatum questionable (knee); Knicks: Full squad
**Situational angle**: Knicks at MSG, Game 3 — home after 0-2 series hole. Backs against the wall.
**Weather**: Indoor — N/A

### Edge Calculation
- My estimated win probability (Knicks cover): 54%
- Implied probability at -110: 52.4%
- Edge: +1.6%
- Kelly sizing: ~3.1% (half-Kelly ~1.5%) → 2 units

### Confidence: Standard

### Result
- **Outcome**: WIN
- **Units**: +1.82 (won at -110)
- **Closing line**: Knicks +4.5 (line moved further against us after bet — CLV +1 point)
- **CLV**: +1 point (we beat the closing line)
- **Notes**: Knicks won outright. Tatum was listed as active but clearly hampered. MSG crowd was a factor from tip-off.
```
