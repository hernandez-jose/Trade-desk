## Core MLB Betting Structure

### Moneyline Dominates
MLB bettors primarily use moneylines (no spread). The "run line" (+1.5 / -1.5) is the spread equivalent.

**Run line dynamics:**
- Dog on run line (+1.5): Much harder to win (they need to keep it within 1 or win outright)
- Favorite on run line (-1.5): Must win by 2+ — value if you think it's a blowout

### Pitching Is Everything
Starting pitching quality is the single most important variable in MLB betting.

**Rule**: Always confirm the confirmed starter before placing a bet. If a starter changes, the line changes — and your analysis changes.

Key starter stats to track:
- ERA (Earned Run Average) — overall quality
- FIP (Fielding Independent Pitching) — true quality without defense
- K% and BB% — strikeout and walk rates (most stable indicators)
- xFIP — expected FIP; corrects for HR rate, most predictive

### Bullpen Rating
A great starter is irrelevant if the bullpen blows it. Track:
- Bullpen ERA (last 14 days)
- Bullpen innings pitched in the last 3 days (fatigue)
- Save/hold situation — does this team have a closer?

## Key MLB Betting Angles

### Starting Pitcher Matchup Edge
When your power rating gives one starter a clear edge:
1. Calculate each starter's true ERA (use FIP or xFIP)
2. Adjust for ballpark (run environment)
3. Adjust for opponent's wOBA (weighted on-base average) vs. LHP or RHP
4. The resulting run-differential is your spread — compare to the moneyline implied probability

### Ballpark Factors
Every stadium has different run environments. Adjust totals accordingly:

| Park Type | Run Environment | Total Adjustment |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Coors Field (Denver) | Extreme hitter's park | +2 to +3 runs vs. neutral |
| Petco Park (San Diego) | Strong pitcher's park | −1 to −1.5 runs |
| Fenway Park (Boston) | Slight hitter's park | +0.5 to +1 run |
| Oracle Park (San Francisco) | Pitcher's park | −0.5 to −1 run |
| Great American Ball Park (Cin) | Hitter's park | +1 to +1.5 runs |

### Lefty/Righty Splits
- Lineups face left-handed pitchers very differently than right-handed pitchers
- A team with 7 right-handed hitters will struggle against a dominant lefty
- Check the opposing lineup's wOBA vs. each handedness

### Weather in MLB
MLB weather matters significantly for totals:
- **Wind blowing out**: Add 1–2 runs to the total; lean over
- **Wind blowing in**: Subtract 1–2 runs; lean under
- **Cold (< 50°F)**: Reduces carry on fly balls; lean under
- **Rain delays**: Can force bullpen games and swing line dramatically

### The Umpire Angle
Home plate umpires have measurable impact on scoring:
- Tight-zone umpires: Pitchers win, lean under
- Wide-zone umpires: Hitters win, lean over
- Track this data for efficiency edges when Klashi doesn't adjust fully

## Totals Strategy in MLB

Totals are set around 8–9 runs for most games. Context adjustments:

```
Base total: 8.5

Adjustments:
  Elite starter vs. weak lineup: −1.5
  Good starter vs. average lineup: −0.5
  Average starter vs. strong lineup: +0.5
  Wind blowing out > 15 mph: +1.5
  Temperature < 45°F: −1.0
  Coors Field: +2.5

Adjusted total: Compare to Klashi's posted number
```

## Series Betting

A team's **series price** (win 2 of 3 or 3 of 4) is often inefficient:
- Sharp bettors exploit when a top ace pitches Game 1 of a 3-game series
- The ace pitches Game 1, the series price doesn't fully account for games 2–3 bullpen battles
- Value: Take the series underdog if their Game 1 ace is the best pitcher in the series
