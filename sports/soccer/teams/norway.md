## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-2-3-1 with Haaland as #9 and Ødegaard behind him

**How they attack:**
Norway's attack is Haaland-centric. Feed Haaland in behind, let him hold up play, or play him into space in the penalty area. Ødegaard is the creative engine — his vision and passing is world-class. On the wings, Norway have direct runners. Set pieces via Ødegaard are a major threat.

**How they defend:**
Mid-block 4-4-2 out of possession. Norway are not the most organized defensively but Solbakken's approach is pragmatic — stay compact and rely on Haaland-led transition attacks. Against equal opposition, they can be vulnerable aerially.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Direct — play to Haaland quickly  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — Ødegaard delivery + Haaland/Sørloth aerial threats  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
