## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with Mbappé as the spearhead and Olise right, Griezmann in the #10 space

**How they attack:**
France's attack is devastating — Mbappé's pace and direct runs in behind stretch every defense. Olise provides dribbling threat on the right. Griezmann links and creates. The midfield engine (Tchouaméni-Kanté-Camavinga/Rabiot) allows the front three to attack freely. Set pieces via Griezmann.

**How they defend:**
Deschamps' organization has evolved — France now press higher than in their 2018 WC win. Tchouaméni anchors and Saliba + Upamecano protect the backline. Maignan is a sweeper-keeper and confident with the ball.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium-High  
**Possession Style:** Balanced — can dominate or counter with Mbappé  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — Griezmann delivery + Giroud-era physical threats now replaced by Mbappé aerial runs  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low
