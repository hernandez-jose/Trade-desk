## Key Tactical Battles

### Battle 1 — Altitude Press vs. DR Congo Energy
Colombia's high-press system becomes even more effective at 1,560m when the opponent isn't altitude-adapted. DR Congo will fatigue faster in the second half — their pressing resistance drops significantly after the 60th minute. Colombia must press early to tire out DR Congo's midfield. **Clear advantage: Colombia, especially in second half.**

### Battle 2 — Yoane Wissa vs. Colombian Backline on Transitions
DR Congo's primary goal threat is Yoane Wissa's pace on the counter. If Colombia leave the fullbacks exposed when pressing, Wissa can run in behind. Colombian fullbacks (Muñoz) are attack-minded — defensive exposure is a real risk. **Advantage: DR Congo on this specific battle.**

### Battle 3 — James Rodríguez vs. DR Congo Midfield Press
James Rodríguez at 35 may fatigue at altitude, but in the first 60 minutes, his vision and passing will be the difference. DR Congo's midfield lacks the technical quality to consistently close James down. **Advantage: Colombia through James.**
