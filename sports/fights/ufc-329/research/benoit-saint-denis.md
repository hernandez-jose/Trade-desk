## Style Notes for This Matchup

### Striking
- BSD is a southpaw left-hand power threat — his left hook is what staggered and would KO orthodox fighters loading on right hands
- Striking accuracy is solid (53%) but he opens up his chin when throwing combinations — Poirier and Moicano both found ways to counter him
- His striking defense is only 41% — the worst among the top 6 LW contenders; he gets hit a lot
- Volume is high (5.17 SLpM) but his primary objective is always to create wrestling opportunities, not stand and trade for 15 minutes
- Against Pimblett (SApM 4.96), a standing battle for 3 rounds is genuinely possible and could be chaotic; neither fighter has elite defensive head movement

### Grappling
- 3.97 TD avg is exceptional — the highest Pimblett will have ever faced
- TD accuracy of 37% is actually moderate, but the sheer volume of attempts will test Pimblett's 100% UFC TD defense
- Sub avg of 0.70/15 min is lower than Pimblett's (1.7) but his submission finishes (11 of 17 wins) show he executes when positions are there
- Arm-triangle choke (Prepolec), face crank (Ruffy), strangulations — diverse submission toolkit
- Will look to grind Pimblett to the mat and punish with elbows and chokes; very similar to what Moicano did to HIM
- Key risk: Pimblett's TD defense (100% UFC) and back attack game — if BSD's shots don't land, Pimblett can scramble offensively
