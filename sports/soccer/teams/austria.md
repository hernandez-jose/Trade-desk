## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-2-3-1 / 4-3-3 under Rangnick — gegenpressing

**How they attack:**
Rangnick's gegenpressing philosophy means Austria win the ball high and attack quickly. Sabitzer's late runs from midfield are a key goal source. Arnautovic holds the ball and links, while Baumgartner operates in the #10 space. Austria are dangerous from set pieces and transitional moments.

**How they defend:**
High-press — Austria aim to win the ball in the opponent's half. Alaba is the quarterback in defense, reading the game brilliantly. The press is intense but can be bypassed by composed build-up play. If the press is beaten, they recover into a 4-4-2.

**Pressing Intensity:** High  
**Possession Style:** Balanced — after winning ball, quick transitions  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium-High  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium — vulnerable when beat by pace in behind
