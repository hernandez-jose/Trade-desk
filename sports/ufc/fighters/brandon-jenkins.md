## Fighting Style

**Flashy pressure striker with switch stance, leg kicks, and spinning techniques**

Jenkins earns his nickname honestly. He is built around entertainment-first striking:
- **Switch stance** — constantly shifting feet, creates angle problems for opponents
- **Leg kick heavy** — targets the lead leg, builds up damage over rounds
- **Spinning techniques** — spinning elbows, spinning back fists, flying knees; high-risk, high-reward
- **Pressure forward** — walks opponents into the fence; does not fight off his back foot

### Striking
- Better output than typical 2.93 SLpM implies — flash and movement inflate his avoidance of jabs and low-investment strikes
- 11 KO/TKO wins is his calling card; when he lands cleanly, he finishes fights
- Accuracy at 41% is below average and reflects the wide variety of flashy attempts
- Absorbs a lot of contact — the style invites exchanges

### Grappling
- **27% takedown defense is catastrophic** — the worst-tier number in the lightweight division
- He is trivially wrestled when opponents commit to the takedown
- Has submission wins (likely from scrambles or front position), but grappling is not his base
- No submission average worth noting offensively

### Durability
- Gets stopped — 5+ TKO/KO losses on his record
- Also has big KO wins, suggesting he plays in dangerous exchanges willingly
- Not a chin-first durability fighter; more of a high-variance brawler
