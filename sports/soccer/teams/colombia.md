## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with James as the #10 and Díaz attacking from the left

**How they attack:**
Colombia's attack centers on Luis Díaz's pace and directness on the left wing. James Rodríguez orchestrates from the #10 position — his vision and passing is world-class even at 35. Daniel Muñoz's overlapping runs from right back create additional width. Richard Ríos makes late runs from midfield. Colombia press high and win the ball in advanced positions.

**How they defend:**
High press under Lorenzo — Colombia close down quickly and aggressively. When the press breaks, they defend in a compact 4-4-2 mid-block. Altitude at Guadalajara (1,560m) may influence fitness management.

**Pressing Intensity:** High  
**Possession Style:** Balanced — technical and dynamic  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium — James delivery + physical attackers  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low-Medium
