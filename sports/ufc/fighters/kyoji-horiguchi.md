## Fighting Style

**Karate-based, ultra-fast technical mixed martial artist; pressure, timing, and quick transitions.**

Karate footwork and blitzing entries; one of the fastest fighters the division has seen. Elite all-around: can strike, wrestle, and submit (RNC of Ulanbekov). Veteran fight IQ and championship pedigree (RIZIN/Bellator titles). Question marks: age 35, two prior KO losses (Asakura, Pettis), and a long absence from UFC competition before late-2025 return.

### Grappling
- Submitted Ulanbekov (RNC R3); credible offensive grappling and scrambles.
- TDD 55% — can be taken down/controlled by strong wrestlers.

### Striking
- Fast, technical, karate angles; 62% striking defense.
- Power exists but has been KO'd twice (durability question at 35).

### Cardio
- Went 3 hard rounds with Albazi to a UD; pace held.
- 5-round cardio at 35 after long layoff is a genuine unknown.
