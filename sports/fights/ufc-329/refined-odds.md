## Methodology

All probabilities derived from three layers blended at fixed weights:
- **CES (Combat Effectiveness Score) — 40%:** Pure first-principles combat metric scoring across 6 domains (Striking, Grappling, Cardio, Physical, Experience, Form)
- **Betting analysis — 40%:** True probability estimates from existing fight-by-fight analysis combining style matchups, market data, and sharp line reading
- **Situational factors — 20%:** Qualitative assessment of camp intel, age, layoffs, momentum, weight class changes, and fight-specific dynamics

American odds converted from refined probability:
- Favorite (>50%): `-(p / (1-p)) × 100`
- Underdog (<50%): `+((1-p) / p) × 100`
- Rounded to nearest 5
