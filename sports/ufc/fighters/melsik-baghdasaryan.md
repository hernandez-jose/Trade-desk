## Fighting Style

**Power-striking kickboxer (southpaw) who hunts the KO.**

Baghdasaryan is a decorated kickboxer (former Wu Lin Feng champion, one-time K-1 welterweight title challenger) who transitioned to MMA. He wants the fight standing, where his southpaw left and kicks generate real KO power. His entire game is built on landing the big shot or out-pointing strikers; he has no offensive grappling and his ground game is a liability.

### Grappling
- No takedowns of his own (0% TD accuracy). Both submission losses came when taken down and controlled.
- Off his back he is vulnerable — submitted by Culibao (RNC) after being grounded.

### Striking
- Genuine southpaw power; finishes via strikes. Good kicks from kickboxing base.
- But 56% striking defense and 4.00 SApM mean he eats shots in firefights; chin was cracked by Jean Silva (R1 TKO).

### Cardio
- Has decision wins (Lutz, Souza) showing he can go 15 minutes, but pace is measured rather than relentless.
