## Domain Breakdowns

### Striking

**Paddy Pimblett — 6.2 / 10**
- SLpM: 5.49 (ufcstats.com) — high volume, aggressive pressure striker
- Striking Accuracy: 52% (ufcstats.com) — slightly above average for UFC LW
- SApM: 4.96 (ufcstats.com) — absorbs significant punishment; 48% striking defense
- Style: Orthodox pressure with jab-cross combinations; poor defensive head movement; stands straight up and down (paddy-pimblett.com/style-and-strengths)

**Benoit Saint-Denis — 6.8 / 10**
- SLpM: 5.17 (ufcstats.com) — strong volume
- Striking Accuracy: 53% (ufcstats.com) — slightly ahead of Pimblett
- SApM: 4.98 (ufcstats.com) — absorbs almost as much as Pimblett; low striking defense: 41% (ufcstats.com)
- Style: Southpaw pressure, dangerous left hand power — KO of Dariush (0:16, UFC 322) via left hook (middleeasy.com, Nov 2025)
- Weakness: 41% striking defense is poor — Poirier KO'd him with a right hook counter in R2 (cbssports.com, Mar 2024); Moicano destroyed him on the ground with elbows
- BSD scores higher than Pimblett here because of the southpaw power advantage, striking accuracy edge, and the fact that Pimblett's head movement is notoriously poor against his preferred left-hand attacks

## Key Analytical Findings

### What the numbers say Pimblett does well:
- **Elite chin and durability:** Never stopped by strikes in 23 professional fights (ufcstats.com / bloodyelbow.com); absorbed brutal 5-round punishment from Gaethje and never went down for good
- **Submission offense when grappling:** 1.7 sub avg per 15 min (ufcstats.com); 10 career sub wins; back attack system is elite — if he scrambles behind BSD, RNC threat is very real
- **Takedown defense (UFC):** 100% TD defense across 7 UFC appearances (ufcstats.com); can survive takedown pressure from high-volume wrestlers

### What the numbers say Pimblett is vulnerable to:
- **Volume striking absorption:** SApM of 4.96 with only 48% striking defense (ufcstats.com); stands straight up with minimal head movement (paddy-pimblett.com); accumulates damage over time
- **Elite wrestling volume:** Has never faced a LW with BSD's 3.97 TD avg; 100% UFC TD defense is impressive but small sample (7 fights) against opponents not approaching BSD's wrestling output
- **Ego-driven game planning:** Abandoned grappling to trade with Gaethje at UFC 324 and lost a fight he may have won via submission (Pimblett's own admission, boxingnews.com); risk he repeats vs. BSD

### What the numbers say Saint-Denis does well:
- **Takedown volume and pressure:** 3.97 TD avg — highest among UFC LW top-10 contenders (ufcstats.com); relentless forward wrestling pressure forces opponents into defensive positions
- **Submission finishing:** 11 of 17 career wins (65%) by submission (ufcstats.com/sherdog.com); diverse submission toolkit including arm-triangle, face crank, RNC; evolved significantly under coach Ott
- **Current momentum:** 4-fight finish streak (Prepolec, Ruffy, Dariush, Hooker) representing the best sustained form of his UFC career; Dariush KO in 0:16 is elite-level proof-of-concept

### What the numbers say Saint-Denis is vulnerable to:
- **Counter-striking KO vulnerability:** Stopped twice in 5 fights (Poirier R2 KO; Moicano doctor's TKO); 41% striking defense is poor among top-10 LW contenders (ufcstats.com); opens his chin when throwing hooks
- **Pimblett's back attack game:** If Pimblett survives early wrestling and scrambles to BSD's back, the RNC threat is legitimate — BSD's 68% TD defense means he can be rolled and put into defensive scrambles
- **Slow starts:** Admitted to starting "sleepy" vs. Moicano (ufc.com post-fight); also cited dehydration issues in the Poirier fight; if Pimblett imposes early submission pressure from bottom or turns a scramble, BSD's slow processing under pressure is a risk
