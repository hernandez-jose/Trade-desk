## Fighting Style

**Disciplined, technically sound counter-kickboxer with elite takedown defense.**

Zahabi is a Tristar-bred technician who fights a low-mistake, high-IQ style: tight defense (71% striking D, 83% TD D), patient counters, and round-stealing in close fights. He does not chase finishes — five of his last six wins are decisions, several of them competitive (split over Vera, controversial UD over Aldo). He has rarely been hurt and is comfortable making fights "quiet," winning the small exchanges and the eyes of the judges. He still owns legitimate one-shot KO power (R1 finishes of Rodriguez and Aoriqileng) if an opponent gets careless.

### Grappling
- Elite takedown defense (83%) from Tristar; rarely planted on his back.
- Offensive wrestling is minimal by choice (0.16 TD/15) — he prefers to strike.
- Sound positional defense; the one career sub loss (Ramos, 2017) predates his current form.

### Striking
- Measured volume (4.06 SLpM) and 46% accuracy — efficiency over flash.
- Strong defensive striking (71%), good chin, counters off a high guard.
- Plans to stand and strike with O'Malley by his own statements; intends a technical kickboxing match.

### Cardio
- Stylistically built for 3 rounds; pulls away late (swept rounds 2–3 vs. Aldo).
- 38 years old — durability/recovery a longer-term watch item, but no recent fade observed.
