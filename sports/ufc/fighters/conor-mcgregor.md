## Style Profile
- **Primary Style:** Counter-striker, southpaw pressure boxer
- **Strengths:**
  - Elite left hand — devastating knockout power at any range
  - Elite timing and distance management, especially at mid-range
  - First-round finishing ability (14 first-round finishes in career)
  - Southpaw stance creates angle complications for orthodox fighters
  - Strong mental game and composure in high-profile settings
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Durability concerns in championship rounds — historically fades after R1–R2
  - Significant strike absorption rate (5.25 SApM) is high — leaves chin exposed
  - Striking defense (51%) is below elite level — can be tagged
  - No significant fights since July 2021 — nearly 5 years of ring rust
  - Grappling exposed at elite level (Khabib, Diaz x2 on ground)
  - Questions over conditioning at welterweight after long layoff
