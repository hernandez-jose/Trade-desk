## Style Profile
Lone'er Kavanagh is primarily a striker with a diverse martial arts background rooted in Taekwondo and kickboxing. He holds a black belt in kickboxing, a Taekwondo black belt (started training age 8), and is a 5x K-1 World Champion before transitioning to MMA. His BJJ is purple belt level — functional grappling for defense rather than offense. His takedown defense is exceptional (88.9% UFC career), and he stuffed all 8 of Brandon Moreno's attempts in their February 2026 title-eliminator.

**Striking:** High-accuracy (49–50%), high-volume (4.29 SLpM) with smart shot selection. Uses footwork, calf kicks, head kicks, and spinning body kicks. Picks shots intelligently rather than relying on pure power. Better defensive striker than Royval (58% def vs 43%).
**Grappling:** BJJ purple belt; functional takedown defense but limited offensive submission game (1 submission win career). Not a threat to submit opponents.
**Cardio:** Showed strong cardio through 5-round performance vs. Moreno. Pace and output remained high throughout.
