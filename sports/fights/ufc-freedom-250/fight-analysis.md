Topuria's 94% takedown defense removes Gaethje's only theoretical escape, and Gaethje barely wrestles (0.33 TD avg) — so the fight stays standing, where Topuria's accuracy and pocket boxing dominate. Topuria already solved a better-defended version of this archetype (Holloway, UFC 308).

### Performance Prediction
- **Most likely (~70%):** Topuria by KO/TKO, Rounds 1–3.
- **Decision branch (~15%):** Topuria outpoints a surviving Gaethje.
- **Gaethje (~15%):** one-shot puncher's chance — his power is real.
