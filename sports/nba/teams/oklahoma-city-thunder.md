## 2025–26 Season Overview

The Thunder are **defending NBA Champions** (beat Indiana Pacers in 7 games in the 2025 Finals) and ran away with the West in 2025–26 at 64–18. They opened the season 24–1 — tying the best 25-game start in NBA history alongside the 2015–16 Warriors. Coach Mark Daigneault won Western Conference Coach of the Month twice this season.

**Offensive identity**: SGA-driven, pick-and-roll system with Holmgren as the roll man. Elite spacing and shot-creation; best player in the world running the show.

**Defensive identity**: Athletic, switchable, deep. Dort is a shutdown wing. Holmgren protects the rim. One of the most complete two-way rosters in the league.

## Betting Profile — Oklahoma City Thunder

### Strengths as a Betting Team
- Defending champions; elite regular-season pedigree (64–18)
- SGA is the most dominant playoff performer in the league right now
- Daigneault system keeps even undermanned rosters competitive
- OKC's first-round playoff record as a franchise: **10–0** entering Game 4
- Third consecutive first-round sweep in reach — organizational motivation locked in

### Weaknesses as a Betting Team
- **Jalen Williams out** — reduces secondary scoring depth
- Winning every game by double digits creates complacency risk in blowout spots (covering large lines late is unreliable)
- Heavy public favorite at all times — books shade lines against them; value is often on the other side
- At -500 ML or heavier, zero mathematical edge — avoid

| Factor | Notes |

### Betting Lean — OKC Going Forward
- Moneyline (-500+) is mathematically unplayable
- Spread covers are the only avenue; however, **winning by 10+ on the road against a desperate team** is historically inconsistent even for dominant teams
- In any future series, watch for the first time OKC faces a team with equal two-way talent — that's when the line value flips back to the Thunder
