## 2025–26 Season Overview

Denver earned the 3-seed in the West on the back of Jokic's continued dominance. However, they've been outplayed by the 6-seed Timberwolves for most of this first-round series. Minnesota's elite defense — particularly Gobert's ability to take away Jokic's interior dominance — has frustrated Denver's half-court system.

The Nuggets hold one meaningful advantage: **home court at altitude**. Ball Arena at 5,280 feet is historically one of the most significant home-court edges in the NBA, affecting opponents' conditioning and shooting in the fourth quarter.

**Offensive identity**: Everything flows through Jokic. He operates as a hub passer and interior scorer. When opponents collapse on Jokic, Murray and MPJ must punish from the perimeter — and they have not done so consistently in this series.

**Defensive identity**: Serviceable but not elite. Gordon provides anchor defense. Susceptible to hot-shooting nights.

### Jokic Game 4 Incident
With 1.3 seconds left in a decided game, Jaden McDaniels made a layup that violated an unwritten NBA rule. Jokic ran the length of the court to confront McDaniels; McDaniels grabbed his jersey.
