# Farid Basharat — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Fight Night: Kape vs. Horiguchi (UFC Vegas 119) | vs. Ethyn Ewing | June 20, 2026 | 135 lbs | 3 Rounds (Prelims)

> Analyst note: Basharat is the more proven, higher-ranked fighter (#15 BW, 15-0, 6-0 UFC) and the better, more reliable grappler. The single most important variable is whether he can impose his chain-wrestling on a strong-based wrestle-boxer; if he can, this is a comfortable control-decision. The market disagrees with the rankings — Basharat is a slight UNDERDOG, which is the headline.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR Basharat:** He is the ranked, undefeated fighter with the deeper résumé (Blackshear, Lapilus, Gutierrez, Matsumoto) and a repeatable, low-variance way to win — chain takedowns, smothering top control, occasional submission threat (6 career subs). Against an opponent whose UFC defensive wrestling is unproven, his grappling is the highest-floor skill in the cage. **AGAINST Basharat:** His striking is non-threatening (1 KO in 15 fights) and his recent wins are razor-thin (split decision over Matsumoto); if Ewing stuffs takedowns and turns it into a striking/clinch brawl, Basharat lacks the power to win that exchange and could be out-pointed or hurt. **Most likely path:** Basharat earns top position, accumulates control time, and grinds out a decision.

## 3. Opponent Context
Ewing (10-2, 2-0 UFC) is a wrestle-boxer with real finishing power and a collegiate wrestling base (King University). That base is the threat: unlike the pure strikers Basharat has controlled, Ewing may have the defensive wrestling and clinch strength to make takedowns costly and force a standing fight — exactly where Basharat is weakest. Ewing also carries upset pedigree (beat unbeaten Wellmaker as a +330 dog, body-shot TKO of Estevam).

## 5. Grappling Analysis

Primary weapon. ~3.5–4 takedowns per fight, chains attempts, strong top control. This is the highest-rated skill in the matchup.

Less tested — his game is to be the grappler, not to defend. If Ewing reverses field, it's uncharted.
### Scrambles & Submissions
6 career submissions; submitted Kleydson Rodrigues via arm-triangle in R1. Real positional sub threat when dominant.
### Cage Wrestling
Uses the fence to chain entries and maintain control; the engine of his decision wins.

## 13. Risk Factors & Confidence Assessment
### Primary Risks to Basharat Winning
1. Ewing stuffs takedowns and turns it into a striking/clinch brawl where Basharat has no power.
2. Ewing's pressure + body work hurts him standing while TDs are denied.
3. Close-decision variance bites (he's lived on the razor's edge lately).

### Confidence Level
Basharat is the higher-floor fighter via grappling, but the path is takedown-dependent and his recent margins are thin. Model estimate (see prediction): **~57% to win**, almost entirely by decision. Given the market has him at ~+108 (≈48% implied), there is a meaningful edge IF the grappling read holds — but Ewing's unproven-but-real wrestling base keeps this in "Low/Standard" confidence, not "High."
