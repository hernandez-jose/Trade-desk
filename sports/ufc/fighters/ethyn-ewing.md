## Fighting Style

**Wrestle-boxer pressure finisher with regional-circuit power**

Ewing blends a collegiate-wrestling base with heavy hands and clinch work. He pressures, mixes takedowns with boxing, and is a genuine finisher — he stopped Wellmaker's hype with a knee in the clinch and TD down the stretch (UFC debut, UD) and brutalized Estevam with a body-shot TKO. Power and pace are real; the open question is whether that translates against an elite control-grappler who can negate his offense from top position.

### Grappling
- King University wrestling background; offensive TD threat and clinch control
- Used a late takedown to seal the Wellmaker decision
- Defensive wrestling vs. a high-level chain-wrestler is unproven

### Striking
- Heavy hands, body work; finished Estevam with a body shot
- Pressure-forward; willing to brawl in the pocket

### Cardio
- Finished Estevam in R3 and won a 3-round decision over Wellmaker — has shown he can push late
