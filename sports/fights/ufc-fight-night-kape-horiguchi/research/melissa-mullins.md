## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR:** Length and reach edge (5'7" / 67" vs Mesquita's ~5'2"–5'4"), a jab-led pressure game (~3.71 SLpM, ~50% acc., reported), reported ~86% takedown defense, and no-gi BJJ credentials (double gold, 2023 IBJJF No-Gi Worlds, brown belt) — so she is not a fish on the mat. **AGAINST:** She has never faced grappling at Mesquita's level; her defense has been compromised closing distance (TKO2 loss to Cornolle); she is off since June 2025 (~12-month layoff); and she is 1–2 in her last three. **Most likely path:** keep it standing on the back foot with the jab, stuff entries, and steal a decision — a narrow, low-probability route.

## 3. Opponent Context (Bia Mesquita)
Mesquita is a 10x IBJJF world champ, 2–0 UFC with two RNC finishes via takedown → back take. She wants the fight on the mat immediately. Mullins' only viable counter is elite takedown defense plus distance striking; if Mesquita gets a body-lock or back, the fight is effectively over. Mullins' no-gi base helps her survive scrambles longer than Mesquita's prior foes but does not close the skill gap.
