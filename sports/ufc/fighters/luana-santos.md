## Fighting Style

**Grappling-first pressure fighter who controls and submits.**

Santos, 26, is a wrestle-grappler who lives off her 2.17 TD/15 offense and an elite 86% takedown defense. She wins by controlling position and chaining to submissions (RNC over Agapova, keylock over Lisboa) or by grinding out control-heavy decisions. Her striking is lower-volume (2.99 SLpM) but defensively sound (64% def, 2.93 SApM). She is young and on a clear upward trajectory.

### Grappling
- Strong offensive wrestling, dominant top control, legit sub threat (1.21 sub/15). 86% TDD means she dictates where it goes.

### Striking
- Functional, low-volume, defensively responsible — used to set up takedowns more than to win firefights.

### Cardio
- Maintains grappling pace across three rounds; control-based output.
