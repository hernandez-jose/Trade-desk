## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 under Bielsa — high-intensity, high-press

**How they attack:**
Under Bielsa, Uruguay presses high and transitions quickly. Darwin Núñez's pace is their primary weapon — he exploits space in behind when the press wins the ball. Valverde's box-to-box running and shooting adds a goal threat from midfield. They're direct and energetic rather than patient.

**How they defend:**
Bielsa's famous pressing game extends to defensive shape. Uruguay press high and aggressively, aiming to win the ball back quickly in the opponent's half. When the press breaks, they recover into a 4-4-2 mid-block. Araújo is outstanding aerially.

**Pressing Intensity:** High  
**Possession Style:** Direct, fast transitions  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low-Medium
