## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 becoming 4-5-1 out of possession

**How they attack:**
Iran plays on quick transitions through Gholizadeh's pace on the right. Azmoun is a physical threat up top who links well. The team is well-organized and disciplined. Against better opponents, they compress space and look for moments in transitions.

**How they defend:**
Compact mid-block that shifts to low-block vs. superior teams. Discipline is their main strength. Pressure comes from organized second lines. Alireza Beiranvand can make crucial saves.

**Pressing Intensity:** Low-Medium  
**Possession Style:** Counter-attack  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium — physical players in the box  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
