# Ion Cutelaba — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Fight Night: Kape vs. Horiguchi (UFC Vegas 119) | vs. Navajo Stirling | June 20, 2026 | 205 lbs | 3 Rounds (main card)

> Analyst note: Cutelaba is a live early-finish underdog whose entire value lives in Round 1. If he doesn't blitz, drag Stirling to the mat, or land the overhand/guillotine inside the first five-plus minutes, the rangy, higher-volume Stirling should out-strike him to a decision. He is a "first round or bust" dog at a price (~+240) that bakes in much of that.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The case FOR Cutelaba: legitimate one-shot power (13 KO wins, 65% finish rate), an explosive blitz, and real grappling/submission threat in scrambles (mounted guillotine over Oumar Sy in March 2026; arm-triangle over Ibo Aslan in Feb 2025). He has finished three of his last four and is fighting in his division, healthy, and on a high. Stirling has never faced a power-puncher of this profile and his chin is untested. The case AGAINST: poor striking defense (~47%), a front-loaded gas tank that fades after R1, and a tendency to over-commit. Stirling is four inches taller with a longer reach, higher output (6.25 vs ~4.44 SLpM), and strong takedown defense (82%) — exactly the profile that survives the early storm and then pulls away. Most likely path for Cutelaba: a Round 1 finish (KO or guillotine); if it reaches R2+, his win equity collapses.

*(Sources: UFC.com, Sherdog, oddsshark.com, cagesidepress.com.)*

## 3. Opponent Context
Stirling is a tall, long, high-volume kickboxer (City Kickboxing) with 82% takedown defense who wants a clean, range-managed three-round striking match. That is the worst kind of fight for Cutelaba past Round 1: he can't easily plant Stirling on the mat (low value vs. 82% TDD), and his ~47% striking defense means he eats Stirling's volume on the way in. Cutelaba's only real lever is to make it ugly and early — close distance, land the overhand or a takedown into a scramble-submission before Stirling settles into rhythm.
