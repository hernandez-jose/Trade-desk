# NBA Situational Trends

## Rest and Schedule Trends

| Situation | ATS Lean | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Team on 0 days rest (b2b road game 2) | Fade | Covers only 44% historically |
| Team on 3+ days rest vs. b2b opponent | Back | ~56% ATS win rate |
| Home team after 2-day rest gap | Back at home | Travel fatigue amplified |
| Resting stars on b2b (load management) | Fade | Public bets the name, not the roster |
| 4th game in 5 nights on the road | Strong fade | Physical exhaustion, lean home team |

## Home/Away Trends

| Situation | ATS Lean |
|-----------|----------|
| Home underdog after 0–2 home record | Bounce-back, back |
| Road team won 5+ in a row | Regression — fade on road |
| Home team after embarrassing loss | Backs-against-wall, back |
| Playoff home team after losing Game 1 | Strong back (57% cover rate) |

## Totals Trends

| Situation | Total Lean |
|-----------|-----------|
| Both teams top-5 in offensive rating | Over |
| Both teams top-5 in defensive rating | Under |
| Fast-pace vs. slow-pace game | Under (slower team controls tempo) |
| Fast-pace vs. fast-pace | Over (4–6 points above standard total) |
| Playoff game 1 of a series | Under (teams adjust defensively to new opponent) |
| Playoff game 6 or 7 (elimination) | Under (pressure reduces offensive output) |
| Back-to-back for one team | Over (fatigued defense = more scoring) |

## Star Player Trends

| Situation | Lean |
|-----------|------|
| Superstar returning from injury | Under their prop (rust); game total push |
| Superstar on revenge game vs. former team | Back the team — but overpriced; reduce unit size |
| Superstar in contract year | Over their props — elevated motivation |
| Superstar out late scratch | Immediate dog; line doesn't adjust fully; back the opponent |

## Coaching Trends

| Situation | Lean |
|-----------|------|
| Elite coach's team after 2+ bad losses | Back — adjustments incoming |
| New coach first-game post-firing | Neutral; 50/50 emotional response |
| Coach known for conservative play: first playoff game | Under — they slow it down |
| Coach known for up-tempo: home playoff game | Over |

## Line Movement Interpretation (NBA-Specific)

- NBA lines typically open Sunday-Thursday for the week's games
- Early line movement on weekday morning = sharp action
- Heavy public betting happens 30–60 minutes before tip-off
- If a line moves against the public % in the final 2 hours: **follow the steam**

## Playoff Round-by-Round Tendencies

### First Round
- Top seeds fade as heavy favorites in Games 1–2 (overhyped)
- Home dogs cover ~51% — slight edge
- Unders hit ~54% in Games 1 and 2 (teams unfamiliar with opponent in playoff setting)

### Second Round
- Lines become tighter and more efficient
- Follow the sharper steam moves only — fading public by itself has less edge
- Rest advantages are magnified (second-round teams have played 4–7 more games)

### Conference Finals / Finals
- Most efficient market — edges nearly disappear
- Props and halftime lines are the best remaining inefficiency
- Public is 80%+ on favorites — significant reverse line movement is rare but meaningful when it occurs
