## Fighting Style

**Pressure striker with a jab and real no-gi grappling credentials.**

Mullins is a late-starting (2016) combat athlete who built into the UFC through IMMAF and a Golden Ticket title, plus genuine no-gi BJJ pedigree (double gold, 2023 IBJJF No-Gi Worlds at brown belt). In MMA she fights primarily on the feet — consistent jab, forward pressure, ground-and-pound when she gets top position. She also has a competent defensive-wrestling base. Her ceiling is a volume striker who can defend takedowns; her floor is the two UFC losses where she was outpointed (Zhelezniakova) and stopped (Cornolle, TKO2).

### Grappling
- No-gi BJJ credentials (brown belt double gold, 2023). Comfortable on the mat.
- Reported strong TD defense (~86%), but untested against an all-time elite grappler.
- Ground-and-pound finishing capability from top.

### Striking
- Jab-led pressure, ~50% accuracy, ~3.71 SLpM (reported).
- Defensive lapses noted when closing distance; was TKO'd by Cornolle.

### Cardio
- Has gone three rounds in the UFC (Alekseeva W, Zhelezniakova L). Functional 15-min gas tank.
