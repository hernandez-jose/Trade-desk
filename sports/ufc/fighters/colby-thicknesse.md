## Background & Credentials

- **BJJ Black Belt** — earned 2022; legitimate grappling pedigree
- **IMMAF Champion** — International MMA Federation, amateur world level competition
- **WKBF Bantamweight Champion** — World Kickboxing and Boxing Federation title
- **National BJJ Champion** — Australian national level
- Started wrestling as a young teen, transitioned to jiu-jitsu, then to full MMA
- Has coached kids' classes at the Windang gym **since age 15** — a coach and competitor simultaneously
- Worked as a security guard before turning professional full-time
- His idol is Alexander Volkanovski — trained beside him during Volkanovski's ascent to UFC champion

## UFC Career Breakdown

### UFC Debut: vs. Aleksandre Topuria — Loss, UD3 (UFC 312, Sydney, Feb 9, 2025)
- Entered **7–0**, undefeated; called up on **short notice**
- Topuria landed a sharp overhead right early in Round 1 and used superior wrestling to dominate
- Thicknesse improved in Round 2 but couldn't score enough

- **Context**: Short notice debut vs. a calculated wrestler is the worst possible matchup — his loss carries an asterisk

- First full training camp fight in the UFC

## Fighting Style

**Grappling-base with striking tools — complete Australian fighter**

Thicknesse is built from the Volkanovski school of Australian MMA: disciplined fundamentals, wrestling backbone, and BJJ submissions layered over it.

### Grappling
- BJJ black belt since 2022 — submission finishes are real threats
- 2 submission wins in 8 professional fights
- 1.0 TD/15 min is moderate volume — not a constant wrestler but uses it strategically
- Takedown defense at 50% is a weakness — can be taken down if opponents commit

### Striking
- 3.33 SLpM is active — throws combinations at a decent rate
- 42% accuracy is below average — needs to sharpen his shot selection
- 2 career KO/TKO wins show he has finishing power in his hands
- Kickboxing credentials (WKBF title) give him legitimate stand-up ability

### Wrestling
- Grew up wrestling — base takedowns are part of his game
- Uses wrestling to get back to his BJJ comfort zone, not to grind for decisions
