## Style Profile
- **Base:** Kickboxing / Muay Thai, with solid wrestling and submission defense
- **Stance:** Orthodox; excellent counter-striker with fast combination work
- **Striking:** High-volume output, crisp jab, sharp low-kick game, accurate head-body combos
- **Grappling:** Strong takedown defense (82% career); can wrestle but prefers to keep fights standing
- **Durability:** Historically durable chin — survived heavy shots from Romero, Adesanya, Du Plessis — though has shown KO vulnerability in later career
- **Cardio:** Excellent stamina over full rounds; trained by coaches at City Kickboxing (Auckland) known for elite conditioning
- **IQ:** High MMA fight IQ; technical, adaptable, utilizes distance management and footwork
