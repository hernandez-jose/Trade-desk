## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR:** Kape (32) is in the best form of his career — three straight KO/TKO wins (Royval R1, Almabayev R3, Bruno Silva R3), elite power (5.12 SLpM, 54% accuracy), strong 77% takedown defense, and he has never been KO'd in 29 pro fights. He's the higher-ranked man (#2 vs Horiguchi's lower seeding) and the fresher, younger athlete. **AGAINST:** Horiguchi already beat him (arm-triangle, R3, RIZIN 2017), owns elite speed and the best fight IQ in the division, and can mix wrestling (TD avg 2.0) into a tactical, distance-controlling game. Kape's losses cluster in close decisions and grappling-heavy gameplans, and he's been submitted twice. **Most likely Kape path:** keep it standing, land the southpaw left, and finish by KO/TKO in rounds 1–3.

## 3. Opponent Context
Horiguchi is the rare opponent who can hurt Kape in every phase: faster hands than most of Kape's recent foes, real wrestling, and proven submission skill (he already armtriangled Kape in 2017 and just RNC'd Ulanbekov). Horiguchi's 55% TDD is beatable but Kape rarely shoots (0.65 TD avg), so Kape won't lean on wrestling. The danger is Horiguchi initiating grappling/clinch exchanges and grinding rounds, or out-speeding Kape on the feet to bank a decision.
