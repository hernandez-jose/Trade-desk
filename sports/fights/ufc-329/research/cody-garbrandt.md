## Injury / Camp Intel
- **Vertigo history:** Garbrandt disclosed that vertigo complications (first noted post-UFC 300 submission loss to Figueiredo, April 2024) resurfaced during and after the loss to Raoni Barcelos (June 2025). He stated it was "managed" heading into the UFC 326 camp (March 2026). *Source: [CBS Sports UFC 326 preview](https://www.cbssports.com/ufc/news/ufc-326-cody-garbrandt-fight-record-title/)*
- **Camp reset:** For UFC 326, Garbrandt described the camp as a deliberate "back to basics" approach — focused on wrestling to control positions and boxing to set traps, designed to simplify his game after years of complex preparation. *Source: [Filmogaz/UFC 326 camp profile](https://www.filmogaz.com/183339)*
- **UFC 329 preparation:** No specific injury reports for the July 2026 camp have emerged. He is presumably healthy following the March 2026 UD win. *Source: [MMA News UFC 329 booking](https://www.mmanews.com/article/cody-garbrandt-returns-at-ufc-329)*
- **Age concern:** At 35, Garbrandt has dealt with extensive injury history throughout his career alongside the vertigo issues; recovery timeline between bouts (March to July 2026 is ~4 months) is adequate.

## Style Notes for This Matchup
- Garbrandt's SLpM of 2.88 is dramatically lower than Yanez's 5.96 — this is a volume mismatch that is dangerous for Cody
- His striking accuracy (40%) and defense (61%) are middle-of-division; Yanez's output will test that defense repeatedly
- Garbrandt's best path to victory is landing the clean counter right hand or left hook before the volume accumulates — he has the power (11 KO/TKO wins, 73.3% finish rate in wins) to end fights with one shot
- 5-inch reach disadvantage vs. Yanez (65" vs. 70") is a major structural problem — Yanez can work from the outside while Garbrandt must close distance
- Takedown defense (78%) is serviceable; if Garbrandt can't get Yanez to engage on his terms, the grappling could be a reset tool
- Historically vulnerable when hit on the chin repeatedly (4 KO/TKO losses in 7 defeats)

## Situational Factors
- **Schedule spot:** 4-month turnaround from UFC 326 (Mar 7) to UFC 329 (Jul 11) — adequate recovery time for a 3-round non-title fight
- **Form:** 2-2 in last 4 with the most recent win aided by opponent point deductions — form is not convincing
- **Age/mileage:** 35 years old, 22 pro fights, multiple devastating KO losses on record; chin is historically questionable at this stage
- **Motivation:** This is likely a must-win to remain relevant in the bantamweight division; a loss could signal the end of a main-card-level run
- **Home turf:** Las Vegas — no travel disadvantage; Garbrandt has fought at T-Mobile Arena multiple times
