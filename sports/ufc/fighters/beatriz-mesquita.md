## Fighting Style

**Elite BJJ grappler who hunts the takedown and the back.**

Mesquita is one of the most decorated gi grapplers in history (10x IBJJF black-belt world champion; Guinness record for most female IBJJF world golds). In MMA she translates that pedigree directly: close distance, secure a takedown or clinch trip, advance position to back/mount, and finish with the rear-naked choke. Both UFC wins followed this exact script. The open question is her striking and her takedown defense if she cannot impose the grappling.

### Grappling
- World-elite. Best-in-division submission threat off the back/mount.
- Both UFC finishes were RNCs after dominant positional advance.

### Striking
- Functional but unproven — used to close distance, not a primary weapon.
- Has not had to stand and trade in the UFC; striking durability untested.

### Cardio
- Has not been past R2 in the UFC; deep-water cardio unproven against a durable foe.
