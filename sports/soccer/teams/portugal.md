## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with Bernardo Silva floating, Ronaldo as target/finisher

**How they attack:**
Portugal's play flows through Bernardo Silva's intelligence. He creates chances with quick combinations and through-balls. Ronaldo occupies center backs aerially and in the box. Rafael Leão provides pace on the left wing. João Neves and Vitinha provide a technical midfield foundation. Set pieces are a major source — Neves delivery + Ronaldo aerial threat.

**How they defend:**
Compact 4-5-1 out of possession. Rúben Dias leads a disciplined backline. Portugal don't press high but close space efficiently. Main vulnerability: quick wide play can expose the fullbacks.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Technical — combination play  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — Ronaldo aerial + Neves delivery  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low-Medium
