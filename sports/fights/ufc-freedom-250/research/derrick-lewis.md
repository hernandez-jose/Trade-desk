# Derrick Lewis — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Freedom 250 | vs. Josh Hokit | June 14, 2026 | 265 lbs (Heavyweight) | 3 Rounds

> Analyst note: Lewis is a 41-year-old, all-time-great KO artist whose path to victory has narrowed to a single overhand right in Round 1. Against a younger, fitter wrestler who can take him down and out-pace him, he is a live R1 puncher's chance and very little else. Bet the R1-KO prop if anything; the straight ML dog price already overrates a fading veteran.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR Lewis:** The most prolific knockout artist in UFC history (16 UFC KOs, ~24 career) with genuine one-punch, fight-ending power and elite composure over a 40+ fight career and two title challenges. Hokit's leaky striking defense (44%, 6.81 SApM) and willingness to brawl give Lewis exactly the firefight he needs — one clean shot at heavyweight ends any night. **AGAINST Lewis:** He is 41 and coming off a Round-2 TKO loss (Cortes-Acosta, Jan 2026); his cardio is notoriously poor (gasses early), his takedown defense is below average (53%), his output is low (2.43 SLpM), and his recent losses (Almeida, Cortes-Acosta) prove that wrestlers and high-pace pressure beat him. Hokit is a D1 All-American wrestler who can take him down and drain him. **Most likely path:** Lewis swings for a Round-1 KO; if it doesn't land, he fades and is finished or clearly out-pointed.

*(Sources: [ESPN – Lewis's UFC knockouts](https://www.espn.com/mma/story/_/id/47674741/derrick-lewis-ufc-knockouts), [MMA Weekly – UFC 324](https://www.mmaweekly.com/news/waldo-cortes-acosta-finishes-derrick-lewis-at-ufc-324), [Wikipedia – Derrick Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Lewis))*

## 3. Opponent Context
Hokit is the worst possible style for an aging Lewis: a high-output, forward-pressure D1 wrestler with a 75% TDD and a real takedown game (3.89/15) against Lewis's 53% TDD and broken cardio. Hokit can either take Lewis down and drain/finish him, or simply out-work him on the feet over three rounds. Lewis's only edge — size/reach and one-punch power — matters most in a Round-1 standing exchange, which is the one phase Hokit can choose to minimize.

## 4. Striking Analysis
### Offense
Low-volume, high-leverage power striking: 2.43 SLpM at 50% accuracy. Lives off the overhand right and uppercut; ~83% of career wins by KO/TKO. One shot ends fights.
### Defense
40% striking defense — below average. He doesn't slip much; he wins exchanges by landing harder, not by not getting hit. Against a volume puncher this is a problem if the KO doesn't come.
### Historical Striking Data
SLpM 2.43 / Acc 50% / SApM 2.43 / Def 40%; TD 0.53/15, TD acc 26%, TDD 53%. *(Source: aggregated UFCStats via [CBS Sports fighter page](https://www.cbssports.com/ufc/fighter/50037046/derrick-lewis/); verify on UFCStats.)*

## 5. Grappling Analysis
### Offensive Wrestling
Essentially none (0.53 TD/15, 26% accuracy). He will not be taking Hokit down.
### Defensive Wrestling
53% TDD — below average and the central vulnerability. Almeida and others have repeatedly grounded him; Hokit's wrestling is a clear level above what Lewis defends well.
### Scrambles & Submissions
Not a submission threat offensively; ground game is survival. Can give up control time and damage from top position.
### Cage Wrestling
Susceptible to being pressed to the fence and worn down — exactly the drain that exposes his cardio.

## 6. Cardio & Durability
### Pace Sustainability
Career-long, well-documented weakness. He visibly gasses, frequently after a single round; his effective danger window is Round 1 and shrinking with age.
### Recovery After Damage
Durable historically, but at 41 and coming off a fresh R2 KO loss, the back-to-back-stoppage risk is elevated.
### Weight Cut Effects
Natural heavyweight, no cut — neutral.
### Injury History
Long career with a documented back issue historically; no new injury reported for this bout. Age-related decline is the bigger factor.

## 10. Historical Context
### Style Matchup History
Lewis vs. wrestlers/pressure fighters with cardio = bad history (Almeida, Cortes-Acosta, Volkov-type pace problems). Lewis vs. stand-and-bang punchers = where his KO reel lives. Hokit is closer to the former category.
### Comparable Opponent Analysis
Cortes-Acosta (Jan 2026) is the most recent and damning comp: timing + a takedown sequence finished Lewis in R2. Hokit's wrestling is stronger than that.
### Career Arc
All-time KO leader in clear physical decline at 41; this is a legacy/spectacle bout, not a contender's fight.

## 11. Key Paths to Victory
### Path 1: Round-1 KO (most likely Lewis win, ~15–18%)
Lands the overhand right/uppercut early while Hokit trades; one shot ends it. This is essentially his entire realistic win equity.
### Path 2: Mid-fight KO if Hokit gets careless (~3–5%)
Hokit over-extends or gets clipped in a scramble/exchange in R2 before Lewis fades completely.
### Path 3: Decision (~1–2%)
Effectively negligible — low output and poor cardio make winning rounds over a high-pace wrestler implausible.

## 12. X-Factors
- **One-punch power** — the great equalizer; never zero at heavyweight.
- **Hokit's defense (44%) + brawling tendency** — gives Lewis more early openings than a disciplined opponent would.
- **Age 41 + post-KO** — elevated chance of an early, decisive fade or a second straight stoppage loss.
- **Motivation** — spectacle bout; question marks over a 41-year-old's urgency in a non-stakes fight.

## 13. Risk Factors & Confidence Assessment
### Primary Risks to Lewis Winning
1. Out-wrestled — 53% TDD vs. a D1 All-American; taken down, controlled, drained, finished.
2. Out-paced — 2.43 SLpM and poor cardio vs. a 9+ SLpM pressure fighter over 15 minutes.
3. Age/decline — coming off a KO loss; back-to-back stoppage risk is real.
### Confidence Level
**Model win estimate: ~20–21% for Lewis** (i.e., Hokit ~79–80%). His equity is almost entirely a Round-1 puncher's chance. At a no-vig implied ~18–20%, the straight Lewis ML carries little to no value (essentially fair, slightly negative after juice). The only Lewis-side play with potential edge is a Round-1 KO prop, where his concentrated win-path probability can beat the priced distribution. Feeds `analysis/mma-fight-prediction.md`.
