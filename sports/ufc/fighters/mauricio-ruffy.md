## Fighting Style

**Dynamic, range-controlling power striker with spinning/capoeira flair.**

Ruffy is a Fighting Nerds product who controls distance with a sharp jab, quick footwork, and powerful straights, then mixes in spinning wheel kicks, wheel kicks, and unorthodox angles (Jiří Procházka-esque wide stance). He fights tall, uses his 75" reach, and converts at an elite finish rate. He absorbs little damage and beats opponents up at range. The clear hole is defensive grappling: his only UFC loss came when Saint Denis took him down inside the first minute and finished him on the mat in Round 2.

### Grappling
- Defensive wrestling is the vulnerability — taken down early and finished by Saint Denis.
- Not a submission threat; offense is essentially all on the feet.
- TD defense reads strong (85%) on paper but was exposed by a high-level grappler.

### Striking
- Elite accuracy (58%), strong power, highlight-reel spinning attacks (KO'd King Green with a spinning wheel kick).
- Uses range and footwork to avoid damage; low absorption.
- Diverse, hard-to-read offense — kicks, spins, straights.

### Cardio
- Built for finishes; most fights end early, so deep-water sample is smaller.
- Showed he can win the late stages of a competitive striking fight vs. Fiziev (R2 TKO).
