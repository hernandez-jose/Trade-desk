## Fighting Style

**Volume pressure striker / brawler with elite leg kicks and serious power — but hittable.**

Gaethje is one of the most violent, high-output strikers in UFC history. He chops opponents down with brutal low/calf kicks, then unloads heavy hooks and overhands. Early-career he ate huge damage in firefights; under Trevor Wittman he became a more measured outside kicker, but his ceiling and floor are both defined by his willingness to trade. His striking defense (51%) and absorption (7.05 SApM) are genuine red flags against precise punchers. Negligible grappling offense; he wins and loses on the feet.

### Grappling
- Wrestling pedigree (NCAA D1 All-American) but rarely shoots — TD avg 0.33.
- 74% takedown defense; decent, not elite. Lost by sub to Oliveira (2022) and was nearly subbed by Khabib.
- No submission game (1 career sub).

### Striking
- Best leg kicks in the division — fight-altering calf/low kicks.
- KO power in both hands (20 KOs); 6.48 SLpM volume.
- Defensive liability: 51% defense, 7.05 SApM — leads with his face in wars.

### Cardio
- Improved markedly under Wittman; went 25 hard minutes vs. Pimblett (UFC 324) at age 37.
- History of fading/getting finished late (Holloway buzzer-beater R5, Khabib R2).
