## Style Profile
Paddy Pimblett is a **grappler-first, pressure striker** whose best weapon is his submission game. He holds a first-degree BJJ black belt and has developed his ground game since age 15. His most dangerous tool is back attacks — he excels at climbing opponents' spines via body locks and scrambles to sink chokes.

On the feet, he is comfortable trading but stands straight up with minimal head movement, making him vulnerable to counter-punchers. He has a notoriously durable chin (never stopped by strikes in 23 fights) but absorbs punishment at a high rate (SApM ~4.96). His takedown defense in the UFC has been elite (100%), though his wrestling offense is modest.

Primary submission threats: rear-naked choke, guillotine, various strangles.
Striking style: high-volume forward pressure, uses jab-cross combinations, limited defensive head movement.
