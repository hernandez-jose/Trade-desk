## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 defensive / 5-4-1 against elite teams

**How they attack:**
Cape Verde is primarily a counter-attacking and set-piece team. They are compact and organized, and on transitions, Ryan Mendes is their most dangerous outlet. Their strength is limiting space and exploiting set-pieces and individual moments. Not a possession team.

**How they defend:**
The 0-0 vs Spain is the best advertisement of their defensive discipline. They organize in a mid-to-low block, compress lanes, and limit shooting opportunities. Their center backs are physically strong and good in the air. Discipline and shape are their primary defensive tools.

**Pressing Intensity:** Low  
**Possession Style:** Counter-attack / set piece  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low (disciplined)
