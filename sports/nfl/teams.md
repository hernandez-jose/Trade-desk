# NFL Team ATS Profiles

> Last updated: April 2026 (pre-season — update after Week 4 of 2026 regular season)
> Format: Team | 2025 ATS Record | Key Notes | Betting Lean

---

## AFC East

### Buffalo Bills
- **2025 ATS**: Track after games begin
- **Profile**: Pass-heavy offense. Strong indoor/outdoor split. Historically fade as large road favorites.
- **Lean**: Bet the under in cold-weather home games; value as dogs >+3.

### Miami Dolphins
- **2025 ATS**: Track after games begin
- **Profile**: Speed-first offense. Legitimate home-field edge at Hard Rock Stadium (heat and humidity disadvantage visitors).
- **Lean**: Fade as road favorites in cold weather; back at home as underdogs.

### New England Patriots
- **2025 ATS**: Track after games begin
- **Profile**: Post-dynasty rebuild period. New coaching regime introduces uncertainty into power ratings.
- **Lean**: Back as division home dogs; fade as road favorites.

### New York Jets
- **2025 ATS**: Track after games begin
- **Profile**: Defense-first identity. Low-scoring games historically.
- **Lean**: Lean under in Jets home games; back as home dogs vs. top offenses.

## AFC North

### Baltimore Ravens
- **Profile**: Elite offensive line; run-first establishes identity. Undervalued late-season.
- **Lean**: Strong late-season ATS trend; back in cold divisional games.

### Cincinnati Bengals
- **Profile**: Explosive passing offense. Inconsistent offensive line protection.
- **Lean**: Fade on the road vs. elite pass rushes; back at home as dogs.

### Cleveland Browns
- **Profile**: Defense-centric. Low-total games. Run-first identity.
- **Lean**: Target unders; back when getting 6+ points.

### Pittsburgh Steelers
- **Profile**: Historically one of the sharpest home-field teams. Divisional games are grinders.
- **Lean**: Back as home dogs; avoid laying points on the road in January.

### Jacksonville Jaguars

### Tennessee Titans

## AFC West

### Kansas City Chiefs
- **Profile**: Consistently one of the most overpriced teams in the market. Public backs them heavily.
- **Lean**: **Fade as road favorites** of more than a field goal. Back division dogs at home vs. KC.

### Las Vegas Raiders
- **Profile**: Historically volatile. New stadium still building home-field identity.
- **Lean**: Avoid betting on. Target teams facing them as road dogs.

### Denver Broncos
- **Profile**: High altitude is the most underrated home-field factor in the NFL.
- **Lean**: Back as home underdogs at altitude; fade opponents traveling to Denver.

### Los Angeles Chargers
- **Profile**: Notoriously underperform as heavy favorites. Lean dog.
- **Lean**: Back the Chargers as dogs; fade them as large favorites.

## NFC East

### Dallas Cowboys
- **Profile**: The most bet-on team in the league. Public inflates their line every week.
- **Lean**: **Fade Cowboys as road favorites** — one of the most reliable fades historically.

### Philadelphia Eagles
- **Profile**: Loud home crowd; physical style. Strong divisional ATS record.
- **Lean**: Back as home dogs; avoid against superior pass rushes on the road.

### New York Giants
- **Profile**: Rebuilding. Monitor QB and OL stability.
- **Lean**: Back as large home dogs (+7 or more).

### Washington Commanders
- **Profile**: Inconsistent. Monitor coaching stability.
- **Lean**: Back as home dogs in division games.

## NFC North

- **Lean**: Back as heavy home underdogs (+6 or more).

## NFC West

### Arizona Cardinals
- **Profile**: Rebuilding. Back as large dogs only.
- **Lean**: +7 or more at home.

### Los Angeles Rams
