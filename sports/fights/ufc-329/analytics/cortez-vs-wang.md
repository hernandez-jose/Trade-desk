**Cong Wang — 9.0/10**

- Assessment: Wang is arguably the most decorated striker in the women's flyweight division by amateur credentials. Her UFC striking numbers confirm elite-level translation. 56% accuracy with 6.22 SLpM is outstanding.

## Final Verdict

> **Cong Wang by Unanimous Decision | CES: 7.43 vs. 6.25 | Edge: +1.18**

The analytics point clearly to Wang. Her striking superiority is not marginal — it is historically elite by UFC flyweight standards, and it is compounded by 100% career takedown defense that eliminates Cortez's only credible path to a win. The grappling domain effectively becomes one-directional: Wang can take the fight down if she chooses; Cortez cannot.

Cortez holds legitimate advantages in UFC experience, has fought at a higher level of opposition, and her cardio is exceptional — she will survive and compete for 15 minutes. But surviving is not winning. Wang's volume (6.22 SLpM at 56% accuracy) will dominate the judges' scorecards even if Cortez avoids the knockout.

The most analytically sound result is Wang winning a clear unanimous decision, likely taking all three rounds on the judges' scorecards. A TKO in Rounds 2-3 is the secondary scenario if Cortez cannot adjust to the striking pace.
