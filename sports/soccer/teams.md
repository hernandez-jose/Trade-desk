## Premier League

### Manchester City
- **Profile**: Historically dominant; overpriced in domestic market after CL exits
- **Home form**: Etihad Stadium — strong but not the loudest atmosphere
- **Lean**: Fade as -250 or worse favorites at home in PL; excellent value as UCL underdogs
- **Rotation risk**: HIGH around European weeks — always check lineup before betting

### Arsenal
- **Profile**: Tactically disciplined; strong home form at Emirates
- **Home form**: Emirates Stadium — solid advantage
- **Lean**: Back as home underdogs when resting key players before a UCL tie; UCL away dog angle in second legs
- **Rotation risk**: MODERATE — Arteta rotates less aggressively

### Liverpool
- **Profile**: Anfield is the most dangerous home environment in English soccer
- **Home form**: Anfield — extreme home advantage
- **Lean**: **Always consider** Liverpool at home as underdogs or pick'em — Anfield effect is real
- **Rotation risk**: MODERATE — heavy European schedule years see more rotation

### Chelsea
- **Profile**: Large squad; rotation makes them unreliable as heavy favorites
- **Home form**: Stamford Bridge — decent advantage
- **Lean**: Fade as heavy chalk; back as dogs when full squad available
- **Rotation risk**: HIGH — one of the heaviest rotators in EPL

### Tottenham Hotspur
- **Profile**: Notoriously inconsistent; public often overcorrects after big wins
- **Home form**: Tottenham Hotspur Stadium — strong, one of the best atmospheres in the PL
- **Lean**: Fade after emotional wins; back after public overreaction to losses
- **Rotation risk**: LOW — smaller squad, rotation is necessity not choice

### Newcastle United
- **Profile**: St. James' Park atmosphere is elite; undervalued in European competition
- **Home form**: St. James' Park — one of the loudest in England
- **Lean**: Back at home as dogs; over lean in home games with an open style
- **Rotation risk**: LOW currently

### Aston Villa
- **Profile**: Emerging club with a consistent tactical identity under Emery
- **Home form**: Villa Park — good advantage
- **Lean**: Back as dogs in UCL; solid home cover rate
- **Rotation risk**: MODERATE around UCL weeks

### Manchester United
- **Profile**: Historically overpriced due to global fanbase; in rebuild
- **Home form**: Old Trafford — currently below its historical standard
- **Lean**: **Fade as favorites** — one of the most reliable fades in recent EPL history
- **Rotation risk**: LOW — need every point; rarely rotate

## La Liga

### Real Madrid
- **Profile**: Santiago Bernabéu is the most dangerous stadium in Europe for upsets
- **Home form**: Bernabéu — extreme advantage
- **Lean**: Back in UCL knockout stages at home; fade as heavy LaLiga favorites when Clasico or Derby coming up
- **Rotation risk**: HIGH around Champions League knockout legs

### FC Barcelona
- **Profile**: Nou Camp provides a significant home advantage; Xavi-era style was high-energy
- **Home form**: Olimpic Lluís Companys (2024–25 while Nou Camp is renovated) — weaker atmosphere
- **Lean**: Adjust line for temporary stadium atmosphere reduction; fade as heavy favorites there
- **Rotation risk**: MODERATE

### Atletico Madrid
- **Profile**: Defensive masterclass under Simeone; known for grinding results
- **Home form**: Civitas Metropolitano — extremely loud and intimidating
- **Lean**: Under lean in all Atletico games; backs-against-wall team in second legs
- **Rotation risk**: LOW — Simeone prioritizes league over Europe

### Athletic Club Bilbao
- **Profile**: San Mamés atmosphere is legendary; physical, high-intensity home games
- **Home form**: San Mamés — elite home advantage
- **Lean**: Back at home vs. visiting big clubs who rotate; under lean
- **Rotation risk**: N/A — they don't play Europe regularly
