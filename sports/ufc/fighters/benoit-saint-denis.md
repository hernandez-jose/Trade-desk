## Style Profile
Benoit Saint-Denis is a **submission specialist and pressure wrestler** with legitimate elite-level grappling credentials. He blends relentless forward pressure with shot selection, forcing opponents to the mat and hunting strangles — particularly arm-triangle and rear-naked chokes. He averages nearly 4 takedowns per 15 minutes (3.97 TD avg), the highest among active UFC LW contenders.

His striking is volume-based and aggressive from southpaw stance. Despite solid accuracy (53%), his defensive striking is poor (41% defense) — he has been stopped by counterpunching twice (Poirier R2 KO, Moicano TKO via doctor's stoppage). He improved significantly under new head coach Nicolas Ott (post-2024 losses), showing more controlled aggression and earlier submission attempts.

Primary submission threats: arm-triangle choke (tapped Prepolec), face crank/strangles (tapped Ruffy), RNC, guillotine.
Striking style: southpaw forward pressure, left hand power, hooks, body work.
