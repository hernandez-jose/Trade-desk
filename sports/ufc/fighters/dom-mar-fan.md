## Background & Credentials

- **BJJ Black Belt** under Team Compton Training Centre — earned August 2025 during his Road to UFC campaign
- Started martial arts training at **age 17** after growing up watching combat sports
- Built his professional record entirely on the **Australian regional circuit** (Eternal MMA and similar promotions)
- Both professional losses came against **Quillan Salkilld** — the Eternal MMA Lightweight Champion and one of Australia's best-credentialed lightweights (also a BJJ black belt, 11-1 record)

- Earned UFC contract at **UFC 325 in Sydney, Australia** — essentially a home-crowd atmosphere for the final

## Fighting Style

**Submission grappler with disciplined defensive striking**

Mar Fan's identity is built around:
1. **Takedown offense** — 62% accuracy is elite; he gets fights to the floor when he wants
2. **Submission threat** — BJJ black belt with RNC and guillotine as primary finishers
3. **Controlled striking** — low volume but high accuracy (54%); he doesn't throw recklessly
4. **Defensive awareness** — 1.47 SApM is outstanding; he closes distance without eating big shots

### Grappling
- Black belt credentials are genuine and recent — earned mid-career at a high level of competition
- Elbows from top position are a weapon — opened Ephoeviga in the Road to UFC
- Ground and pound is a realistic finishing path, not just a stalling tool
- Submission entries: RNC (back control) and guillotine (front headlock entry) are primary threats

### Striking
- 54% accuracy suggests he's a selective, measured striker — not a volume puncher
- Uses striking primarily to enter grappling range
- 2.27 SLpM is low — he is not trying to win striking battles, he's trying to get the fight down
- Power in his hands is present but not his primary weapon (1 career KO/TKO)
