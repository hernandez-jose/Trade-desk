## Bout 1 — Main Event | Welterweight (170 lbs) | 5 Rounds

### Fight Context

McGregor makes his return after a 5-year absence (last fought July 10, 2021 — TKO loss, broken leg, vs. Poirier). He was cleared from a USADA anti-doping suspension in March 2026. This is a rematch of their UFC 196 bout (March 2016), which McGregor won by unanimous decision. Both fighters are coming off losses: McGregor hasn't won since 2020 (Cerrone), while Holloway lost to Charles Oliveira at UFC 326 (March 2026) by UD 50-45 on all three cards.

Unusually, **both fighters are moving up in weight** — McGregor vacated lightweight and Holloway is a natural featherweight/lightweight jumping to 170.
