## Final Verdict

### ✅ Norway vs. Senegal

This is the most mispriced match of Matchday 2 in our model. Norway at −165 implies a 62% win probability, but our 5-step composite model gives Norway only 45% — a 17-percentage-point gap. The reasons: (1) this is a genuinely competitive matchup between two teams with similar composite ratings (7.65 vs. 6.50); (2) Senegal's must-win desperation adds meaningful motivation (+5% probability shift); (3) Mané and Ismaïla Sarr give Senegal genuine goal-scoring threat; (4) Norway's defensive line can be exposed by pace. The betting market has over-weighted Haaland's star power and Norway's comfortable 4-1 MD1 result. The sharp play is to fade Norway's moneyline through Senegal +0.5 AH, which covers any non-Norway-win outcome (draw or Senegal win) at +130. Additionally, the Over 2.5 and BTTS markets offer strong value — Senegal must attack, creating transition opportunities for Haaland.
