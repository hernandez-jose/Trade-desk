## Fighting Style

**Explosive southpaw knockout striker with strong takedown defense.**

Kape is one of the most dangerous strikers in the flyweight division — explosive footwork, precise power shots, and elite finishing instinct. He fights at distance, looks to create knockout openings, and has a 64% KO rate. Boxing/BJJ base. His weakness historically has been losing close decisions and being controlled/out-grappled by elite wrestlers; he has never been finished by strikes.

### Grappling
- 77% TDD keeps strikers' fights standing; BJJ base gives him scramble awareness.
- Has been submitted twice in career (incl. by Horiguchi); can be controlled by elite grapplers (Mokaev decision loss).

### Striking
- Knockout power in both hands, dynamic southpaw angles, sharp counters. 3-fight KO/TKO streak (Royval, Almabayev, Bruno Silva).

### Cardio
- Mostly finishes early; 5-round cardio less tested but went 25 minutes vs Mokaev.
