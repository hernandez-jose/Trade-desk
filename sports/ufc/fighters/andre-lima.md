## Fighting Style

**Pressure striker built around volume leg kicks with a credible scramble/grappling layer.**

Lima walks opponents down behind a high-output leg-kick game and pressure boxing, accumulating damage and points over three rounds. He is undefeated and has shown he can win on the feet (dos Santos, Raposo) and finish on the mat (Barez RNC), giving him paths in multiple phases. Composed and durable for a young prospect; has not been seriously hurt in the UFC.

### Grappling
- Solid scrambles and offensive grappling; finished Barez with a rear-naked choke in R3.
- Decent takedown success (~71% accuracy on a small sample).

### Striking
- Signature weapon is the leg kick — high volume, disrupts opponents' footwork and offense.
- ~58% striking accuracy and ~58% defense (small sample) suggest clean, controlled output.

### Cardio
- Has gone to decision four-plus times; output holds into R3. Pressure pace appears sustainable over 15 minutes.
