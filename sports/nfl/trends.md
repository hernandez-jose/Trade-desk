# NFL Situational Trends

> These are statistically validated trends over 10+ seasons. Use as confirmation signals, not standalone picks.

## Spread Trends

### Underdog Trends
| Situation | ATS Win % (Historical) | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Home dog +3 to +6 | 53% | Most reliable underdog spot |
| Home dog +7 or more | 57% | Books sometimes overcorrect |
| Division dog any venue | 52% | Familiarity neutralizes talent gap |
| Dog after 2+ straight ATS losses | 54% | Books and public punish losers too much |
| Playoff dog at home | 56% | Road favorites historically underperform |

### Favorite Trends
| Situation | ATS Win % (Historical) | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Favorite off a loss | 48% | Public over-adjusts to motivate narrative |
| Favorite in TNF as road team | 45% | Short rest, road, prime time = sell |
| Favorite > -10 overall | 44% | Sharp faders hit blowout resistance |
| Reigning Super Bowl champ as regular season fave | 47% | Hangover effect documented |

## Total Trends

| Situation | Over/Under Lean | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| Wind > 20 mph | Under | Each 5 mph above 15 costs ~1 point |
| Temperature < 25°F | Under | Ball doesn't carry; hand warmers slow tempo |
| Two top-5 scoring defenses | Under | Sample: 58% under rate |
| Two bottom-10 scoring defenses | Over | 56% over rate |
| Both QBs healthy in a dome | Over | 54% over rate |
| Game after a bye week (both teams) | Under | Coaches default to conservative opening game plan |

## Divisional Game Trends

- **Totals**: Division games go under more often (52%) — defensive familiarity
- **Spread**: Dogs cover more often (52%) — talent gap is neutralized by film
- **Primetime division games**: Public inflates favorites; reverse line movement often fades the favorite

## Schedule-Based Trends

### Short Week (Thursday Night Football)
| Situation | Lean |
|-----------|------|
| Road team on short rest (played Sunday) | Fade — covers only 44% |
| Home team on short rest (played Sunday) | Neutral — slight home edge |
| Home team had Mon/Tue game vs. opponent's Sun game | Back home team |

### Bye-Week Edges
| Situation | Lean |
|-----------|------|
| Team coming off bye as underdog | Back — covers 55% |
| Team coming off bye as heavy favorite (>-7) | Neutral — no consistent edge |
| Opponent is on short rest while you had the bye | Strong back |

### West Coast Bias
- West coast teams traveling east for early (1pm ET) kick: Cover rate drops ~4%
- East coast teams traveling west for late (4:25pm ET) games: Slight disadvantage
- Night games: Time zone disadvantage largely neutralized

## Revenge and Motivation Trends

| Situation | Lean |
|-----------|------|
| Revenge game (lost to this opponent 1–12 months ago, blowout) | Lean toward motivated team |
| Team eliminated from playoff contention (must-win for opponent) | Neutral — "dead rubber" games unpredictable |
| Week 17 resting starters | Avoid betting — teams managing load |
| AFC/NFC championship rematch regular season | Over-hyped, fade the narrative |

## Primetime Appearance Bias

Teams in prime time more than 3× in the first half of the season:
- Typically overpriced due to public attention
- Lean against these teams as road favorites
- These teams' totals are often inflated — lean the under

## Power Rankings vs. Efficiency Metrics

When raw win-loss records diverge from efficiency metrics (EPA, DVOA, PFF grades):
- Regress toward the efficiency metric for future bets
- Historically, teams with good efficiency but poor records bounce back ATS in weeks 6–12
- Teams with bad efficiency but good records regress ATS in the same window

---

> **Trend Reliability Note**: No trend wins 100% of the time. These are probability tilts of 3–7%. Combine 2–3 signals in the same direction before boosting bet size. A single trend by itself is 1-unit territory; three overlapping trends pointing the same way justify 2–3 units.
