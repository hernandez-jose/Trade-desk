## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 / 4-2-3-1 with De Bruyne as the pivotal creator

**How they attack:**
Belgium's attack is built around De Bruyne's vision. He drives from deep, finds Lukaku or Openda as the focal point, and creates through combination play and set pieces. On the flanks, Carrasco provides direct running. When Lukaku starts, Belgium play direct; with Openda, they play on the counter with pace in behind.

**How they defend:**
Mid-block 4-4-2 out of possession, pressing when opponents try to play out from the back. Courtois is the last line and world-class. Defensive line can be exposed by quick transitions.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Balanced — quick transitions preferred  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — De Bruyne delivery is elite  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
