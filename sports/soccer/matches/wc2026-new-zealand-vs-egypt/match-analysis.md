### Battle 3 — Egypt Set Pieces vs. NZ Aerial Defense
Egypt excel at dead-ball situations. NZ are physically decent but outmatched by Egypt's set-piece threats. Ashour's MDI goal vs Belgium came from a dead ball. **Advantage: Egypt.**
