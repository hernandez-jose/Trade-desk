## Fighting Style

**Tall southpaw submission grappler ("The D'Arce Knight") who hunts chokes off the clinch and scrambles.**

Daukaus is a long, rangy Brazilian jiu-jitsu specialist who wins fights by initiating grappling exchanges and snatching the D'Arce choke (at least 6 career D'Arce finishes). His best work comes when opponents engage him in the clinch or on the mat. His striking is functional but defensively leaky (43% striking defense, 3.58 SApM), and both of his UFC losses (Dolidze, Anders) came by KO/TKO. His takedown defense (83%) is genuinely strong, which keeps him in his preferred phase. The fight he wants: a willing grappling partner he can entangle and choke.

### Grappling
- Elite D'Arce/front-headlock game; opportunistic, fast chokes (50s on Meerschaert at UFC 322).
- Strong takedown defense (83%) — hard to hold down, fights back to scrambles.
- High submission rate (1.64 sub/15min). Dangerous off the back and in transitions.
- **Concern:** offensive takedown success is low (~24%); he needs opponents to come to him.

### Striking
- Functional southpaw output (3.04 SLpM), recently showed power (43s KO of Pereira).
- **Concern:** porous defense (43%, 3.58 SApM); both UFC losses are KO/TKO; suffered facial fractures vs. Dolidze.

### Cardio
- Comfortable into later rounds (D'Arce of Fallon in R3 at CFFC). Recent UFC wins ended in <1 minute, so deep-water UFC cardio is less recently tested but historically adequate.
