## Core Soccer Bet Types

### 1. 1X2 (Match Result)
- **1**: Home win
- **X**: Draw
- **2**: Away win

This is the foundation of soccer betting. The draw makes soccer unique — home teams don't always have the probability edge they do in other sports.

### 2. Asian Handicap
Asian handicaps eliminate the draw, giving half-ball or full-ball advantages:
- **−0.5**: Home team must win; draw counts as a loss
- **+0.5**: Away team wins or draws
- **−1**: Home team must win by 2+; if win by 1, bet pushes
- **+1**: Away team wins or draws; if lose by 1, bet pushes

Asian handicap is recommended over 1X2 because it eliminates the draw variable and often has better juice.

### 3. Over/Under Goals
Standard is 2.5 goals (over 2.5 = at least 3 goals scored).  
Common markets: 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5 goals.

### 4. Both Teams to Score (BTTS)
- Yes: Both teams score at least 1 goal
- No: At least one team finishes with 0 goals

## Soccer Betting Angles

### Home-Field Advantage
Soccer home advantage is more pronounced than in other sports (~60% home win rate across top leagues).  
Factors:
- Travel distance (especially in Champions League)
- Crowd noise and referee subconscious bias (studies show home teams get more favorable calls)
- Pitch familiarity

### Draw Probability
The draw is the most underbet outcome. Public bettors consistently undervalue it:
- Expected draw rate: ~25–28% across all top-league games
- Books price it at implied ~24–26% — slight value on draws in well-matched games
- **Fade the draw when**: One team is a clear technical superior or the game is must-win for both

### Late-Season Motivation Tiers
End of season creates 4 distinct motivation categories:

| Team Situation | Motivation Level | Betting Impact |
|---------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Fighting for the title | Extreme | Cover/win at higher rate |
| Locked into position (mid-table) | Low | Dead rubber — unreliable |
| Chasing European spot | High | Extra motivation |
| Fighting relegation | High | Backs-against-wall |
| Playing a rotation squad (Cup/European game 3 days prior) | Very Low | Fade |

### Squad Rotation Risk
Big clubs rotate heavily around European fixtures. A Premier League club playing UCL Wednesday will rotate for the Saturday league game:
- Their true "strength" for that game is 15–20% lower
- Books sometimes don't fully adjust
- **Fade** rotated big clubs as heavy favorites in domestic games around European weeks

## Champions League Specific Angles

### Away Goal History (No Longer in Effect)
Note: UEFA eliminated the away goal rule. Away goals no longer count double in two-legged ties. This changes second-leg dynamics:
- A 1–0 first-leg lead no longer guarantees the leader will be conservative at home in Leg 2
- Trailing teams take more risks since there's no away goal penalty
- **Lean over** in second legs where the aggregate score is close

### Quarter-Final / Semi-Final Trends
- First legs are typically cautious — under 2.5 goals hits ~58% historically
- Second legs (when a team is eliminated or close) produce more open play — over 2.5 hits ~55%
- Home team in a one-goal deficit in Leg 2: **must attack** → lean over and home win
