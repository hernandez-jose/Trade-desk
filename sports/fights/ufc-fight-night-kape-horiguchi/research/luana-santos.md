## 1. Executive Summary
The case FOR Santos: 26 years old, 5–1 UFC, riding a multi-fight win streak, elite takedown defense (86%), active wrestling (2.17 TD/15), and a real submission game (RNC over Agapova, keylock over Lisboa). The case AGAINST: she is stepping up from No. 14 to a ranked No. 8 veteran, her striking volume is low (2.99 SLpM), and if Rosa's 68% TDD keeps the fight standing, Santos can be out-volumed on the cards. ## 3. Opponent Context
Karol Rosa (19–7, 8–4 UFC, No. 8) is a high-volume pressure striker (6.49 SLpM, 56% accuracy) but defensively leaky (49% striking defense, 4.91 SApM) and not a grappler (1.20 TD/15, 68% TDD). Rosa threatens Santos by keeping it standing and burying her in volume. But Rosa's 68% TDD is beatable, and once on the mat she is not a submission or control threat — exactly the terrain Santos wants.
