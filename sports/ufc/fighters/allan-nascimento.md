## Fighting Style

**Submission grappler / opportunistic finisher with calculated striking.**

Nascimento is a high-level BJJ grappler who hunts the finish — 15 of his wins are submissions. He picks his striking (61% accuracy) and uses length, but the win condition is getting to the mat or the clinch and attacking the neck (anaconda, RNC). He does not need to be the one who initiates the takedown; he is dangerous off scrambles and from top or back control.

### Grappling
- Elite submission hunter, especially front-headlock/anaconda and back takes.
- Low offensive TD numbers — wins grappling exchanges via scrambles and opportunism, not double-leg volume.

### Striking
- Accurate, measured, uses reach. Not a volume KO threat (1 career KO).

### Cardio
- Goes the distance comfortably (multiple 3-round decisions); long layoffs from cancellations, not durability.
