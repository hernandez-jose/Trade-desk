# Ethyn Ewing — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Fight Night: Kape vs. Horiguchi (UFC Vegas 119) | vs. Farid Basharat | June 20, 2026 | 135 lbs | 3 Rounds (Prelims)

> Analyst note: Ewing is the live, dangerous underdog-by-narrative who the market has flipped to a slight favorite (~-125). ## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR Ewing:** A collegiate wrestler with genuine pop and a finisher's instinct — he upset unbeaten Malcolm Wellmaker (as a +330 dog, on ~2 days' notice) and brutalized Rafael Estevam with a body-shot TKO in his second UFC fight. If he keeps it standing, he wins the striking and has the power to finish. His wrestling base gives him a credible chance to stuff Basharat's takedowns and force the fight he wants. **AGAINST Ewing:** Only a 2-fight UFC sample (data is noise), and he has never faced a chain-wrestler of Basharat's caliber. If Basharat gets him down and rides position, Ewing's offense is neutralized and he loses rounds. **Most likely path:** Defend takedowns, pressure forward, and either out-strike for a decision or land a fight-ending shot — but it hinges on keeping it off the mat.

*(Sources: [SI — Wellmaker upset](https://www.si.com/fannation/mma/news/ethyn-ewing-upsets-malcolm-wellmaker-2-days-notice-ufc-322), [Yahoo — Estevam TKO](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/ufc-vegas-115-ethyn-ewing-014530463.html), [MMA Mania](https://www.mmamania.com/ufc-fight-announcements/441619/top-prospect-ethyn-ewing-collides-with-another-undefeated-fighter-at-ufc-vegas-119))*

## 3. Opponent Context
Basharat (15-0, #15) is a chain-wrestling control grappler with ~4 TD/fight, 6 career subs, and a low finish rate at UFC level (wins by decision). He threatens Ewing exactly where Ewing is least proven: takedown defense and bottom-position survival against an elite controller. Conversely, Basharat's lack of power (1 KO in 15) means if Ewing keeps it standing, he is in little KO danger and holds the striking edge.

## 13. Risk Factors & Confidence Assessment
### Primary Risks to Ewing Winning
1. Basharat gets repeated takedowns and rides position for control-decision rounds.
2. Ewing's bottom game / scramble defense cracks; gives up a sub.
3. Two-fight sample masks holes a chain-wrestler can exploit.

### Confidence Level
Ewing is the higher-ceiling, lower-floor side. He genuinely can win standing and the market backs him, but his case rests on takedown defense that has zero high-level UFC proof. Model estimate (see prediction): **~43% to win**, mostly via decision/TKO if it stays standing. At ~-125 (≈55.6% implied), the market is pricing him ABOVE the model — i.e., no value on Ewing's moneyline; if anything the edge is on Basharat.
