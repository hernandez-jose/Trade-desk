## 1. Executive Summary
The case FOR Stirling: four-inch height and ~4-inch reach edge, much higher output (6.25 vs ~4.44 SLpM) at better accuracy (52% vs 43%), low strikes absorbed (2.67 SApM), elite takedown defense (82%) that neutralizes Cutelaba's wrestling lever, and proven three-round composure (three UFC decision wins) plus a recent finish (R2 TKO of Bruno Lopes, Mar 2026). He gets to fight exactly the fight he wants: long-range volume striking. The case AGAINST: small UFC sample (4 fights), an untested chin against true one-shot LHW power, no ranked/elite win yet, and a fast-starting opponent who is most dangerous in the very first round where adversity is most likely. Most likely path: survive/avoid the early blitz, then out-volume Cutelaba to a clear decision or late TKO as the veteran fades.

*(Sources: UFC.com, Sherdog, RNZ, oddsshark.com, cagesidepress.com.)*

## 3. Opponent Context
Cutelaba is an explosive, front-loaded wrestle-brawler with real one-shot power and scramble-submission threat, but a poor gas tank, ~47% striking defense, and low fight IQ.
