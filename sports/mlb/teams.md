## American League East

### New York Yankees
- **Betting profile**: Most bet-on MLB team; consistently overpriced at home
- **Park**: Yankee Stadium — slight hitter's park, right-center power alley helps lefties
- **Lean**: Fade as heavy home moneyline favorites (-160 or worse); back as underdogs

### Boston Red Sox
- **Betting profile**: High-profile but often mispriced when rotation is thin
- **Park**: Fenway Park — hitter's park, Green Monster inflates lefty pull hitting
- **Lean**: Over lean at Fenway with weak pitching matchups; monitor bullpen health

### Baltimore Orioles
- **Betting profile**: Strong young core; undervalued by casual public
- **Park**: Camden Yards — neutral, slight hitter's lean
- **Lean**: Back as home underdogs vs. weak starters

### Tampa Bay Rays
- **Betting profile**: System team that consistently outperforms payroll; opener strategy experts
- **Park**: Tropicana Field (dome) — neutral run environment
- **Lean**: Trust the Rays as home dogs; under lean when they use the opener

### Toronto Blue Jays
- **Betting profile**: Power-hitting lineup; struggles with elite lefties
- **Park**: Rogers Centre (dome) — offense-friendly
- **Lean**: Over when facing weak RHP; under vs. dominant LHP
