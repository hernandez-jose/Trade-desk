## Style Profile
Brandon Royval is a BJJ black belt who serves as a grappling instructor at Factory X. His style is chaotic, high-volume, and scramble-based. He draws opponents into firefights and exploits openings for submission transitions. His southpaw stance creates awkward angles. He is one of the elite scramblers in the UFC flyweight division, capable of turning defensive positions into sudden offensive submission attempts. His striking uses constant movement, feints, and angle-creation rather than power-first output. His chin and defensive striking metrics are below divisional average — he gets hit frequently in exchange-heavy bouts, a pattern that has cost him in close fights and against knockout threats.

**Grappling:** BJJ black belt; 9 career submission wins including arm triangles, guillotines, armbars, and triangle chokes. His submission rate of 52% of wins is elite for the division.
**Striking:** Southpaw pressure striker; high volume but low accuracy (38%). Generates most damage in scrambles and clinch transitions rather than clean exchanges.
**Cardio:** Exceptional. Fights at championship pace across 5-round bouts; holds Fight of the Night record for the UFC flyweight division with 5 bonuses.
