## Style Notes for This Matchup

### What Bautista Does Well vs. Sandhagen
- **Submission output:** 4 UFC submissions (tied 3rd-most in BW history) — if he can close distance and threaten from clinch/ground, he creates danger even for a submission-aware Sandhagen. Source: [Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/fighter/Mario-Bautista-128107)
- **Switch stance:** Bautista's southpaw/orthodox switching disrupts rhythm and changes the striking angles Sandhagen has prepared for. This was not a feature of their 2019 meeting when Bautista was a raw UFC debutant
- **Volume output:** 5.33 SLpM ties Sandhagen output-for-output; Bautista's 47% accuracy is slightly better than Sandhagen's 44% — he lands a higher percentage of what he throws
- **Revenge motivation:** Only professional loss came from Sandhagen — 7 years of preparation and significant stylistic evolution since the 2019 armbar loss
- **Grappling growth:** Patchy Mix (himself a top submission grappler) was handled by Bautista in June 2025 — shows significant grappling development

### Potential Vulnerabilities
- **Range disadvantage:** 2" reach and 2" height disadvantage against Sandhagen; must close distance through Sandhagen's danger zone
- **Striking defense:** 54% sig. strike defense (below division average for ranked fighters) — absorbs 3.91 SApM, meaning Sandhagen's counter-striking will land
- **Grappling exposure against elite submissions:** Lost to Sandhagen's armbar in 2019; Sandhagen's overall submission IQ is higher than Bautista's grappling defense
- **TD accuracy:** 33% TD accuracy is low; against Sandhagen's footwork and cage awareness, landing clean takedowns will be difficult

## Situational Factors
- **Rest:** ~8 months since UFC 321 loss — full recovery expected
- **Motivation:** Maximum — only career loss to Sandhagen; avenging a submission loss is a powerful psychological driver
- **Narrative:** "7 years in the making rematch" — high-profile platform on International Fight Week PPV (UFC 329)
- **Experience gap:** 17–3 vs. 18–6; Bautista has limited PPV main card experience; UFC 329 is a significant step up in profile
- **3-round format:** Cuts against Bautista's submission hunting style; fewer rounds limits his ability to wear Sandhagen down before finishing
- **Style evolution since 2019:** Bautista was 0 professional fights into his UFC career in 2019 — he has 11 UFC fights, 3 Performance of the Night bonuses, and a contender-level record since then
