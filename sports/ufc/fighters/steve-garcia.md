## Fighting Style

**Southpaw knockout artist — long, rangy power striker who stuffs takedowns and ends fights early.**

Garcia is a high-volume, high-accuracy southpaw with serious one-shot power (79% of wins by KO/TKO). His length (75" reach, 6'0" at 145) plus 88% takedown defense let him dictate range and keep fights standing, where his SApM of 2.45 and 59% defense show he is also hard to touch. The questions are whether his grappling holds up against an elite black belt and whether he can finish a durable opponent who survives the early storm.

### Grappling
- Defense-first: 88% TDD, Jackson-Wink scramble fundamentals.
- No offensive submission game (0 career sub wins); not looking to grapple.

### Striking
- Southpaw, long, accurate (49%), 5.39 SLpM with knockout power.
- Recent run is all first-round stoppages — extremely dangerous early.

### Cardio
- Mostly unproven at distance lately because fights end early; older fighter (34) but durable, low damage absorbed.
