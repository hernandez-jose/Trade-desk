## Fighting Style

**Submission-oriented Brazilian heavyweight finisher (low-sample regional prospect).**

Big-bodied (6'4") Brazilian HW out of Curitiba with a 100% finish rate, leaning heavily on submissions (4 of 6 wins) plus knockout power (2). Profile is that of a grappler who can also bang. Major caveats: tiny and inconsistent career sample (6–1 over a decade with long layoffs), never tested at UFC level, and his lone loss came to a Contender Series-caliber opponent (Jacobi). All ratings below are tentative given the data gap.

### Grappling
- Apparent strength — submission-first finisher. Specific positions/subs unverified.

### Striking
- Two KO wins suggest real HW pop, but technique/volume unproven against quality.

### Cardio
- Untested deep into fights (everything has ended early); 3-round cardio is an open question vs. UFC pace.
