## 1. Executive Summary
**For:** Lima is 11–0 (4–0 UFC), out-strikes most flyweights with a high-volume leg-kick game (4.06 SLpM), and has shown he can finish on the mat (RNC of Barez). Most likely path: Lima out-volumes and out-positions Borjas for a clear decision, with a live finish if Borjas's durability cracks again.

## 3. Opponent Context
Borjas (1–4 UFC) is a regional-honed pressure fighter who has been overwhelmed by UFC pace and stopped twice (Costa, Imanol Rodriguez).
