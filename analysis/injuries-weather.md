# Injuries & Weather — Situational Impact Guide

## Injury Analysis Framework

Not all injuries are equal. A star QB missing changes everything. A backup offensive lineman missing changes almost nothing. Learn to quickly assess impact.

### Impact Tiers by Sport

#### NFL
| Position | Impact Level | Line Adjustment |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Starting QB | Extreme | 7–14 points |
| Left Tackle (pass-blocking) | High | 2–3 points |
| WR1 or TE1 | Moderate | 1–2 points |
| Running Back (bell cow) | Moderate | 1–2 points |
| Cornerback (CB1 vs elite WR) | Moderate | 1–2 points |
| Pass rusher (DPOY-level) | Moderate | 1 point |
| Backup OL | Low | 0–0.5 points |
| Special teams player | Minimal | 0 |

**Critical rule**: If a QB changes, re-evaluate the entire bet from scratch. Backup QBs average 6–8 fewer points per game than starters.

#### NBA
| Position | Impact Level | Line Adjustment |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Primary star (25+ PPG) | Extreme | 8–12 points |
| Secondary star (18–24 PPG) | High | 4–6 points |
| Starting PG (offense orchestrator) | Moderate | 2–4 points |
| Starting C (defensive anchor) | Moderate | 2–3 points |
| 6th man (key rotation player) | Low | 1–2 points |
| Bench player | Minimal | 0–1 point |

**Critical rule**: When a star sits on a back-to-back (load management), the public still bets the team name. Fade these teams hard.

#### MLB
| Factor | Impact Level | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Starting pitcher change | Extreme | Re-evaluate entire bet |
| Closer unavailable | High | Affects save situation and totals |
| CF/SS (defensive anchor) | Moderate | Affects pitcher ERA |
| Cleanup hitter out | Moderate | Affects run scoring potential |
| Lineup position changes | Low | Often absorbed by the lineup |

**Critical rule**: If the confirmed starter changes, the bet has no action on most books — but verify Klashi's rules. Some allow action on any pitcher.

#### Soccer
| Player Type | Impact Level | Notes |
|-------------|-------------|-------|
| #9 / main striker | High | Reduces scoring probability by ~15–20% |
| Creative midfielder (primary playmaker) | High | Reduces chance creation |
| GK (starting vs backup) | High | Goalkeeping quality gap is largest in soccer |
| CB partnership disruption | Moderate | Affects clean sheet probability |
| Full back (overlap specialist) | Low-Moderate | Affects wide play |

## Where to Get Injury Information

### NFL
- **Official**: NFL.com injury report (Wednesday — initial; Friday — final)
- **Beat reporters**: Team-specific X/Twitter accounts for day-of practice updates
- **Practice report**: DNP (Did Not Practice) = likely out; LP (Limited) = game-time decision; FP (Full Practice) = likely to play

### NBA
- **Official**: NBA.com injury report (updated 1 hour before tip-off)
- **Beat reporters**: Team beat reporters on X — often know before official report
- **Coach pregame availability**: Coaches answer injury questions ~90 min before tip

### MLB
- **Official**: MLB.com starting lineups (usually released 3–4 hours before first pitch)
- **Beat reporters**: On X, they know if a starter is being skipped before the lineup card is official
- **Bullpen rest**: Track pitchers used in last 2–3 days; a bullpen using 3+ pitchers in back-to-back days is fatigued

### Soccer
- **Official**: Club press conferences (48 hours before match)
- **Beat reporters**: Sky Sports, The Athletic, L'Equipe (for French clubs)
- **Training ground reports**: Watch for "didn't train" vs. "trained with the squad"

## Timing Your Injury Bet

The earlier you act on an injury, the more edge you capture:

```
Timeline:
  Day before game: Injury reported → Best time to bet before line adjusts
  Morning of game: Klashi adjusts but usually not fully → Still some edge
  1 hour before: Line has nearly fully adjusted → Edge mostly gone
  30 minutes before: Fully adjusted → No edge from injury alone
```

When a star player is injured after markets open:
1. Check Klashi immediately — the line may not have moved yet
2. Calculate the adjusted line based on the impact tier above
3. If Klashi's line doesn't reflect the injury: bet the adjusted side
4. If Klashi has already moved the line fully: pass

## Weather Analysis

### NFL Weather

Wind is the most important weather variable in outdoor sports:

| Wind Speed | Impact | Betting Action |
|------------|--------|----------------|
| 0–10 mph | Negligible | No adjustment |
| 11–15 mph | Minor | −0.5 from total |
| 16–20 mph | Moderate | −1.5 from total; lean under |
| 21–25 mph | Significant | −2.5 from total; strong under |
| 26–30 mph | Major | −3.5 to −4; very strong under |
| 30+ mph | Extreme | −5+; both teams should run the ball |

**Direction matters**: Crosswind = field goal issues. Headwind into the open end = punting and scoring reduction. Tailwind for the offense = slight over lean.

Temperature:

| Temperature | Impact |
|-------------|--------|
| 50°F–70°F | No adjustment |
| 35°F–49°F | −0.5 from total; consider −1 |
| 25°F–34°F | −1.5 to −2; ball-handling issues begin |
| Below 25°F | −2.5+; significant passing reduction |

Rain/snow:

| Condition | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Light rain | −0.5 from total |
| Heavy rain | −1.5 to −2; both rushing teams gain advantage |
| Light snow | −1 from total; QB accuracy slightly reduced |
| Heavy snow | −2 to −3; run-heavy game expected |

**Combined weather**: Stack multiple factors. If it's 22°F AND 25 mph wind AND snowing: this is a −5 to −7 total game. If the book only dropped the total 2 points, hammer the under.

### MLB Weather

Wind direction at the ballpark is the critical factor:

| Situation | Lean |
|-----------|------|
| Wind blowing OUT (to CF or power alleys) at >12 mph | Over |
| Wind blowing IN (from CF) at >12 mph | Under |
| Crosswind at >15 mph | Slight under (harder to control at-bat) |
| Temperature <50°F | Under (ball doesn't carry) |
| Temperature >90°F | Slight over (ball carries in heat) |
| Humidity >85% | Slight over (damp air, ball carries) |
| Rain delay during game | Adjust for bullpen game dynamics |

**Wrigley Field** deserves special mention: The wind at Wrigley changes everything. Always check the rooftop wind before betting any Cubs home game.

**Coors Field**: Altitude adjustment always applies (+2–3 runs), but wind DOES matter additionally. Wind blowing out at Coors is among the most powerful over signals in baseball.

### Soccer Weather

Weather is less critical in soccer than in outdoor American sports but still matters:

| Condition | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Heavy rain | Slicker ball, more slips, slightly more chaotic — lean over (more errors) |
| Strong wind | Reduces long-ball effectiveness; disrupts aerial game |
| Extreme cold | Players tighten up in early minutes — lean under in first half |
| Extreme heat | Pace slows significantly in 2nd half; more rotation, lean under 2H |
| Artificial turf after rain | Ball moves faster — slightly over lean |

## Pre-Game Checklist

Before placing any bet, run through this checklist:

```
INJURIES:
[ ] Starting pitcher confirmed (MLB)
[ ] Starting QB confirmed (NFL)
[ ] Star player active (NBA/Soccer)
[ ] Any "game-time decision" players that would change your pick
[ ] Klashi's line reflects the known injury fully

WEATHER (outdoor games only):
[ ] Wind speed and direction checked
[ ] Temperature checked
[ ] Precipitation forecast reviewed
[ ] Combined weather factors calculated
[ ] My total adjustment factored into the pick decision
```
