## Fighting Style

**Kickboxing-based heavyweight striker with one-shot KO power.**

Diniz is a long, rangy ex-GLORY kickboxer who wants the fight standing at distance, behind a jab and heavy hooks/kicks. He has legitimate heavyweight pop (left hook KO of Austen Lane) and good output for the division. His weaknesses are squarely on the mat: both UFC losses came from opponents getting him to the canvas / into clinch-and-ground exchanges (Pinto finished him from full mount). Wins by attrition striking against limited opposition; vulnerable to grapplers and pressure.

### Grappling
- Minimal offensive wrestling; no submission game. Defensive grappling is the clear hole — finished from mount by Pinto.

### Striking
- Primary weapon. Long frame (79.5" reach), kickboxing footwork, real KO power in the hands. Best self when keeping range.

### Cardio
- Kickboxing base gives decent gas tank for 3 rounds; can fade if forced to grapple/scramble.
