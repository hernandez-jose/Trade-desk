## Style Notes for This Matchup
- **Striking edge:** Kavanagh's 4.29 SLpM and 50% accuracy vastly outperforms Royval's 38% accuracy. His 58% defense also significantly outperforms Royval's 43% — the cleaner striker and harder to hit
- **Calf kick game:** Used calf kicks to diminish Moreno's movement; same strategy applicable vs. Royval who relies on footwork and angle-creation
- **Takedown defense wall:** 88.9% TDD is among the best in the UFC flyweight division. Royval's primary grappling entry is scramble-based rather than double-legs — Kavanagh may struggle more with clinch drags than with pure shot attempts
- **KO vulnerability:** Johnson exposed a flaw: when hurt inside on the feet, Kavanagh can be stopped. Royval's scramble-based style could create those tight exchanges where flash KOs happen
- **Submission defense:** Purple belt BJJ — functional but unproven against elite black belt grapplers. If dragged into deep submissions, this is the question mark. Career 88.9% TD defense shields this vulnerability well
- **5-round fight capacity:** Proved cardio and output hold up in 5 rounds vs. Moreno (this fight is 3 rounds — less of a concern)
