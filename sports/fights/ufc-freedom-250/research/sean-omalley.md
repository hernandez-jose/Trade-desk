# Sean O'Malley — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Freedom 250 (White House, Washington D.C.) | vs. Aiemann Zahabi | June 14, 2026 | 135 lbs | 3 Rounds

> Analyst note: O'Malley is the rare heavy favorite whose primary liability (elite wrestling) is precisely the skill Zahabi has chosen NOT to use. A striker-vs-striker fight plays directly to O'Malley's length, accuracy, and power. The fight he loses (a 25-minute mauling) is not the fight on offer here.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The case FOR O'Malley: he is the longer (72" vs 68"), more accurate (61% vs 46%), higher-volume (6.70 vs 4.06 SLpM) striker, and Zahabi has publicly committed to standing and striking — surrendering the one dimension (wrestling) that beat O'Malley twice against Merab Dvalishvili. In a pure kickboxing match O'Malley controls range with the jab and front kick and lands the straight right as Zahabi closes. The case AGAINST: Zahabi is defensively excellent (71% striking defense, rarely hurt), is on a 7-fight win streak built on stealing close rounds, and O'Malley's measured, counter-reliant pace can be made "quiet" by a disciplined opponent who blunts his rhythm — exactly Zahabi's stated plan. The most likely path: O'Malley out-points Zahabi at range over three rounds for a clear decision, with a non-trivial KO/TKO branch if Zahabi over-commits.

*(Sources: UFC.com, Wikipedia, Verdict MMA, MMA Mania preview, Covers/BetMGM previews.)*

## 3. Opponent Context
Zahabi (5'8", 68" reach, orthodox, Tristar) is a disciplined counter-kickboxer with elite takedown defense (83%) and strong striking defense (71%). Critically, he has stated he intends to strike with O'Malley rather than wrestle. That decision removes O'Malley's only proven kryptonite. What Zahabi *can* do to threaten O'Malley: make the fight low-output and ugly, win the small exchanges, and lean on the judges in a close three-rounder — the formula that earned him split/contested decisions over Vera and Aldo. He is also durable and unlikely to be blown out early, so O'Malley likely has to earn this over 15 minutes rather than starch him.

## 4. Striking Analysis
### Offense
Long-range sniper: 6.70 SLpM at 61% accuracy — elite for 135. Jab and teep control distance; the straight right and counter check-hook are the finishers (63% career KO rate). Switch stance creates alignment problems for an orthodox opponent. He is happy to fight off the back foot and counter a pressuring foe.
### Defense
60% striking defense, generally good head movement and distance management. He has been hurt — the 2020 Vera loss came via a leg kick that disabled his peroneal nerve, and he absorbs a moderate 3.48 SApM. Chin is good but not granite.
### Historical Striking Data
SLpM 6.70 | Accuracy 61% | SApM 3.48 | Striking defense 60% (UFCStats / UFC.com). The volume and accuracy edges over Zahabi (4.06 / 46%) are the statistical core of the matchup.

## 5. Grappling Analysis
### Offensive Wrestling
Negligible (~0.1–0.3 TD/15). O'Malley does not shoot; he is a pure striker. Not a factor here since Zahabi also won't wrestle.
### Defensive Wrestling
The career question mark. Serviceable vs. non-wrestlers, but Dvalishvili took him down repeatedly and controlled both fights. Against Zahabi this is largely moot — Zahabi shoots almost never (0.16 TD/15, 14% accuracy) and has declared a striking gameplan.
### Scrambles & Submissions
Limited offensive grappling; survival-based off his back. The one career path to a Zahabi win on the mat (Tristar pressure + a scramble) is unlikely given Zahabi's stated intent and weak takedown offense.
### Cage Wrestling
Not a strength, but unlikely to be tested in a striker-vs-striker fight.

## 11. Key Paths to Victory
### Path 1: Range decision (most likely, ~50%)
Jab, teep, and leg kicks bank rounds; counter the right hand when Zahabi enters; clear unanimous decision over three.
### Path 2: KO/TKO (~30%)
Zahabi over-commits or stands in the pocket; O'Malley lands the straight right/counter and finishes or accumulates a late stoppage.
### Path 3: Damage-based grind (~5%)
Leg kicks and body work visibly slow Zahabi; O'Malley pours it on late for a one-sided final round (and possible doctor/ref stop).

## 13. Risk Factors & Confidence Assessment
### Primary Risks to O'Malley Winning
1. He fights too passively/counter-heavy and Zahabi steals two close rounds on the cards (Zahabi's proven formula).
2. A surprise Zahabi takedown/clinch-grind despite his stated plan (low probability given 0.16 TD/15).
3. Variance — Zahabi's latent KO power lands clean.
### Confidence Level
This is a clean style read in O'Malley's favor: a striker-vs-striker fight where he holds the reach, accuracy, volume, power, and big-stage edges, with his lone weakness (wrestling) neutralized by the opponent's chosen gameplan. Model estimate for O'Malley: **~82% to win** (capped near the model's high-confidence ceiling for a durable, defensively-sound opponent who can extend it to the judges). Most likely outcome: O'Malley by decision. The ML at -400/-475 (implied ~80–82.6%) offers essentially no edge; value, if any, is on O'Malley-by-decision at +100.
