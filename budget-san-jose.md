## San Jose-Specific Cost Pressures

### Labor (Biggest Risk)
California has the highest restaurant labor costs in the US.

| Role | Hourly (2025–2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Line cook | $20–$26 | SJ market rate above CA minimum |
| Prep cook | $18–$22 | |
| Dishwasher | $17–$20 | |
| Server | $16.50 + tips | CA minimum; tips are extra |
| Barista / cashier | $17–$21 | |
| Sous chef | $25–$35 | |
| Manager | $65K–$90K/year | |

**California employer add-ons:** Payroll taxes, workers' comp, paid sick leave (40–80 hrs/year), meal/rest break compliance. Budget **+20–25%** on top of wages for employer burden.

AI scheduling and cross-training staff minimizes overtime — this is where the AI stack pays for itself fastest.

### California-Specific Regulations That Affect Budget
- **PAGA (Private Attorneys General Act):** Wage/hour compliance is litigated aggressively. Budget for an HR audit before opening.
- **Cal/OSHA:** Kitchen safety compliance; factor into build-out.
- **Prop 22 / Delivery platforms:** 25–30% commission fees eat into revenue; negotiate or build in-house ordering.
- **AB 1228 (FAST Recovery Act):** $20/hr minimum for fast food chains with 60+ locations nationally — does not apply to independent restaurants, but watch for local ordinances.
- **San Jose Minimum Wage:** Currently tracks to CA state minimum; verify current rate at sanjoseca.gov.

## Revenue Targets & Profitability Model

### Fast Casual Example (1,200 sqft, 50 seats)
| Metric | Conservative | Target | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lunch covers/day | 60 | 90 | 120 |
| Dinner covers/day | 40 | 60 | 80 |
| Average check | $18 | $22 | $25 |
| Daily revenue | $1,800 | $3,300 | $5,000 |
| Monthly revenue | $54,000 | $99,000 | $150,000 |
| Annual revenue | $648K | $1.19M | $1.8M |

**Break-even:** At ~$80K/month revenue with controlled food (30%) and labor (32%) costs.

### Adding Corporate Catering (San Jose Advantage)
One corporate catering contract (e.g., weekly lunch for 50 tech employees) adds:
- 50 covers × $18 avg = $900 per event
- Weekly = $3,600/month from one account
- 5 accounts = $18,000/month incremental with minimal extra overhead

This is the highest-margin revenue stream available in San Jose. Target startups and mid-size tech companies within 3 miles.

## Funding Sources

| Source | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Personal / Friends & Family | $50K–$200K | Fastest, no equity required |
| SBA 7(a) Loan | $150K–$500K | 10–25 year term, 7–9% rate; requires business plan + collateral |
| SBA Microloan | Up to $50K | Faster approval, good for ghost kitchen phase |
| SBDC (Bay Area SBDC) | Free consulting + loan referrals | Free resource, use it |
| City of SJ Small Business Loan | $10K–$100K | Check sanjoseca.gov/business |
| Restaurant365 / Bluevine LOC | $50K–$250K | Revenue-based, faster than SBA |
| Angel Investor / Equity | Varies | Dilutive; only if you have high-growth concept |
| Crowdfunding (Mainvest, Honeycomb) | $50K–$500K | Community investment, also marketing tool |

**Recommended path:** Start with personal capital + SBA microloan for ghost kitchen phase. Use revenue to fund fast-casual build-out with SBA 7(a) after 12 months of proof.
