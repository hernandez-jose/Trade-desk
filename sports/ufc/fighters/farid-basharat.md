## Fighting Style

**Grappling-first control wrestler with submission threat — "mini-Khabib" comparison**

Basharat wins by closing distance, chaining takedowns (near 4/fight), and controlling on top for long stretches, mixing in submission attempts. His striking is functional but not a finishing weapon at UFC level; his last four UFC wins are decisions built on top control and volume grappling.

### Grappling
- Primary path to victory; high takedown volume and tight top control
- 6 career submissions — real threat once on the mat
- Khabib-style smothering control reputation

### Striking
- Functional, used to set up entries; not a power threat (1 KO in 15)
- Defensive striking is respectable (~57% def)

### Cardio
- Has gone three full rounds repeatedly in UFC; grind-it-out style implies a solid tank
