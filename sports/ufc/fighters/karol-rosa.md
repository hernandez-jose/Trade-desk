## Fighting Style

**High-volume pressure boxer who wins rounds on output.**

Rosa pushes forward and throws in volume, leaning on a 6.49 SLpM rate and 56% accuracy to bank rounds. She is durable and almost always in three-round decisions. The flip side: she absorbs nearly 5 sig strikes/min and her 49% striking defense means a sharper or busier striker can outpoint her, and a grappler can steal rounds with control.

### Grappling
- 68% takedown defense keeps most fights standing; offensively a non-factor (1.20 TD/15).

### Striking
- Forward pressure, volume combinations, good chin. Hittable defensively.

### Cardio
- Reliable three-round gas tank; comfortable in decisions.
