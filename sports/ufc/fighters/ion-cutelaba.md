## Fighting Style

**Explosive wrestle-brawler — all-or-nothing early blitzer**

Cutelaba opens like a freight train: huge first-round output, takedown attempts, and overhand power. He is one of the most dangerous early-finishers at 205 when he lands clean or gets a guillotine/arm-triangle off the scramble. The flip side: his striking defense is poor, his gas tank and discipline fade if he doesn't finish early, and he is hittable when he wades in.

### Grappling
- Strong takedown entries off the blitz; 57% TD accuracy, 76% TD defense.
- Real submission threat in transitions (mounted guillotine vs. Sy; arm-triangle vs. Aslan).

### Striking
- Heavy hands, overhand right, wild bursts. Power over precision (43% accuracy, ~47% defense).
- Leaks strikes; SApM ~3.34.

### Cardio
- Front-loaded. Output and composure drop sharply after R1 if no finish. Avg fight time 7:58 reflects early endings (his wins and losses).
