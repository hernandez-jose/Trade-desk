## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with deep press triggers and positional play

**How they attack:**
Spain's possession game is the most sophisticated at the tournament. They circulate through Rodri at the base, with Pedri and Gavi making dynamic runs into half-spaces. Lamine Yamal on the right cuts inside to shoot or link with Morata. Left-sided attacks come from fullback overlaps. Set pieces are a secondary source — Carvajal and Laporte offer aerial threat.

**How they defend:**
High press in a 4-3-3 block. Morata leads the press from the front. When the press is broken, they drop into a compact 4-5-1 mid-block and deny space in behind. The back four is technically assured — rarely exposed by direct play. Main risk: fast transitions if press is beaten.

**Pressing Intensity:** High  
**Possession Style:** Dominant (typically 60%+ possession)  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low
