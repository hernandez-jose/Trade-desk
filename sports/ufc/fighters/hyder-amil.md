## Fighting Style

**High-volume, high-output pressure striker ("The Hurricane") with elite cardio and a leaky grappling game.**

Amil pushes a relentless volume-striking pace and wins by burying opponents in output across three rounds, with real one-shot power (6 of 11 wins by KO/TKO). The two-fight skid exposed the ceiling: he was iced in 26 seconds by a sharper, harder hitter (Delgado) and then out-wrestled/out-pointed by a fighter who mixed in takedowns (Emmers). His path is on the feet at range; his vulnerability is anyone who can change levels and control him.

### Grappling
- Thin offensively (1 career sub). Defensive wrestling is the clear hole — conceded 7 takedowns and 8:35 of control to Emmers; gets neutralized when forced to grapple.

### Striking
- High volume, switches stance, busy combinations, decent power. Primary and best phase.

### Cardio
- Elite engine — built to push pace for 15 minutes; rarely fades.
