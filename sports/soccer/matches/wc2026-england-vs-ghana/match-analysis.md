## Key Tactical Battles

### Battle 1 — Mohammed Kudus vs. England Midfield
Kudus is Ghana's primary danger — his pace and dribbling can unlock any defense in transition. England's Bellingham and Rice in midfield are elite, but Kudus' athleticism creates problems. **Slight advantage: Ghana on this specific battle.**

### Battle 2 — Harry Kane vs. Ghana's Central Defense
Kane is in sublime form — 2 goals vs Croatia. Ghana's defenders (Amartey, Salisu, Djiku) are physical but can struggle against world-class movement. Kane will get chances. **Advantage: England.**

### Battle 3 — England's Wide Attack vs. Ghana's Full-backs
England's Saka (right) and Rashford/Foden (left) will pin Ghana's full-backs. Ghana will be forced to defend deep. This creates the conditions for England to control possession and create from wide areas. **Advantage: England.**
