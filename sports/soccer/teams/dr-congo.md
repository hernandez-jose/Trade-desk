## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with Wissa as the primary goal threat

**How they attack:**
DR Congo attacks on transitions and through Yoane Wissa's pace. They are direct and physical, exploiting space behind defenses. Wissa's ability in 1v1 situations and his pace make him a constant threat. Altitude at Guadalajara may impact their energy levels in the second half.

**How they defend:**
Mid-block 4-4-2 / 4-5-1. Disciplined but can be pressured by sustained attacking from quality teams. Chancel Mbemba's physicality is central to their defensive structure.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Counter-attack  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium — physical players  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
