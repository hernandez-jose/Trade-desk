## Fighting Style

**Elite-credentialed wrestler-grappler with developing, accurate, low-volume striking.**

Bo Nickal is a 3× NCAA Division I champion and 4× NCAA finalist out of Penn State — one of the most decorated collegiate wrestlers ever to enter the UFC. His MMA path is built on dragging opponents to the mat with explosive level changes, controlling top position, and hunting submissions (arm-triangle, guillotine). On the feet he is patient, accurate, and rangy, but his output is low and he had historically not been tested against a high-level pressure striker until de Ridder. The fight he wants: takedown early, top control, finish.

### Grappling
- Generational wrestling pedigree (3× NCAA champ). Chain wrestling, mat returns, and top pressure are his core.
- Multiple submission finishes (arm-triangle, sub of Brundage). Active sub-hunter from top.
- **Concern:** de Ridder neutralized his grappling and out-positioned him in the clinch — the first time a UFC opponent did so. Whether that was a style/skill issue or a one-off is the open question.

### Striking
- Accurate (62%) and low-volume (2.76 SLpM). Big one-shot power flashed in the Vieira head-kick KO.
- Defensively sound on paper (0.55 SApM, 75% def) but those numbers come from short fights where he was rarely under sustained pressure.
- **Concern:** absorbed clean head/body strikes and a fight-ending knee vs. de Ridder; striking defense under pressure is unproven.

### Cardio
- Most fights end early, so deep-water cardio is largely untested. Wrestling base implies a solid aerobic floor, but he has limited Round 3 data (only the Craig and Vieira fights reached it).
