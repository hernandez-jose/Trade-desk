## 2025–26 Season Overview

The Suns entered the 2026 playoffs via the **play-in tournament for the first time in franchise history** — a significant step back from the contender-window teams built around Durant, Booker, and Chris Paul. The Kevin Durant trade reshaped the roster around Booker and Jalen Green, but the team never found consistent two-way performance during the regular season.

First-year head coach Jordan Ott (elevated from the Cleveland Cavaliers staff) was thrown into a difficult situation: rebuilding on the fly with a new system and an injury-riddled roster.

**Offensive identity**: Heavily Booker-dependent. Jalen Green is the secondary creator but his injury management is a constant variable. No true point guard infrastructure — Booker handles much of the playmaking.

**Defensive identity**: Inconsistent. Dillon Brooks provides one capable wing defender but the team lacks the depth and athleticism to guard elite offenses.

## Betting Profile — Phoenix Suns

### Strengths as a Betting Team
- **Back-against-the-wall spot** — teams facing elimination at home cover at higher rates historically
- Booker is capable of an explosive performance when desperate (season-or-sweep narrative)
- Home court for Games 4 and a potential 5 — crowd energy is a real factor
- Play-in teams play loose; no expectations pressure
- Sharp bettors have moved juice on PHX to -120 (dog side), signaling professional money on the Suns to cover

### Weaknesses as a Betting Team
- This roster is genuinely inferior — no Kevin Durant, injury questions at multiple positions
- Outscored by an average of **~20 points per game** across the first three games
- Jalen Green injury management reduces second-unit firepower

- Zero defensive answer for SGA — no one on the Suns can guard him
