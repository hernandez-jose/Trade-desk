# Beatriz "Bia" Mesquita — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Fight Night: Kape vs. Horiguchi (UFC Vegas 119) | vs. Melissa Mullins | June 20, 2026 | 135 lbs | 3 Rounds (Prelims)

> Analyst note: Mesquita is an all-time-great BJJ grappler who has won both UFC fights via rear-naked choke inside two rounds. She is the #15-ranked bantamweight and a heavy favorite. ## 1. Executive Summary
**FOR:** Mesquita is a 10x IBJJF world champion (Guinness record for female golds) translating directly to MMA — both UFC wins are takedown → back/mount → RNC, finishing Alekseeva (R2) and Montserrat Rendon (R1). She is the only ranked fighter in the bout (#15) and the clear grappling superior. **AGAINST:** Only 7 pro fights, debuted at 33 (now 35), striking is functional-only, and her cardio/durability beyond R2 is unproven. If a rangy, pressure-striking opponent stuffs takedowns and keeps it standing, she has no demonstrated Plan B. **Most likely path:** close distance, secure a takedown, advance position, finish by RNC in R1–R2.

*(Sources: Wikipedia, SI/FanNation, UFC.com, Sherdog)*

## 3. Opponent Context (Melissa Mullins)
Mullins is a 5'7" pressure striker with reported ~86% takedown defense and real no-gi BJJ credentials — exactly the profile that could trouble Mesquita IF the TD-defense number holds against an elite level she has never faced. Mullins' threat is to keep the fight standing, use her jab and length, and avoid the back-take. Her risk: defensive lapses closing distance (TKO loss to Cornolle) and zero experience against world-championship-caliber grappling.
