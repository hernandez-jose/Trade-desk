## Fighting Style

**Well-rounded wrestler-grappler who scores off scrambles and submissions.**

High-school wrestling base adapted into a well-rounded MMA game. Uses quick striking combinations to create entries, then shoots and looks to advance position or attack submissions. Low striking output (SLpM 1.73) means he wins rounds with grappling and control rather than volume on the feet. Has shown a durable, scrappy approach but his last two losses were both close decisions where he was out-pointed.

### Grappling
- Excellent takedown conversion (100% cited), strong scrambles, 3.0 sub avg — genuine finishing threat on the mat.
- TD defense is a weakness (43%) — can be controlled by stronger wrestlers (Costa).

### Striking
- Low volume, ~43% accuracy. Uses strikes to set up the shot more than to win exchanges. Defense (49%) leaves him hittable against high-output strikers.

### Cardio
- Has gone three full rounds repeatedly; competitive into round 3 in recent losses. Solid but not elite gas tank.
