## Style Profile
- **Primary:** Muay Thai / Kickboxing with high-level submission grappling
- **Striking:** Elite range management, long jab, flying knees, spinning attacks, creative combination striking. Exceptional counter-striker who times entries with precision.
- **Grappling:** Dangerous submission grappler — holds BJJ brown belt. Guillotine and armbar specialist. Can also wrestle but prefers submission offense when taken down.
- **Defense:** Uses lateral footwork and head movement to avoid exchanges. Strong clinch defense.
- **Weaknesses:** Can be taken down by elite wrestlers; limited top control once taken to the mat; cardio questioned in title fight against Dvalishvili (UFC 320).
