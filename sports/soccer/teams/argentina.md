## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 with Messi floating from right to central, Álvarez as the #9

**How they attack:**
Everything flows through Messi. He drops deep to receive, drives at defenders, and creates chances from seemingly nothing. Álvarez's runs in behind — often while Messi holds the ball — create the primary goalscoring channel. De Paul and Enzo Fernández provide the midfield engine. Argentina attack through all channels with quality.

**How they defend:**
Argentina has a compact 4-4-2 defensive shape. They don't press as aggressively as the top European teams but Scaloni has built a disciplined defensive unit. Lisandro Martínez is aggressive in the CB role. Emiliano Martínez is elite in 1v1 and penalty situations.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Balanced — can dominate or counter  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — Messi free kicks + Álvarez aerial ability  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low
