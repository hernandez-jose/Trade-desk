## Fighting Style

**Rangy, light-footed technical kickboxer — an "MMA point-fighter" who outpoints with movement and volume**

Gane is the most graceful mover in the heavyweight division. He fights on the outside, uses constant lateral movement, switches stances, and peppers with a high-volume but low-power jab/kick attack. He rarely takes damage (2.25 SApM, 63% defense) and is built to win five-round decisions by frustrating and out-touching bigger, slower punchers.

### Grappling
- Light wrestling (0.62 TD/15) used to mix up looks rather than control.
- TD defense (56%) is the hole in his game — Jon Jones took him down and submitted him in R1; he can be controlled by a credentialed wrestler.
- 3 career sub wins show some finishing ability on top, but he is a striker first.

### Striking
- Elite footwork and distance management; best defensive heavyweight striker in the UFC.
- High-volume but notably **low one-punch power for a heavyweight** — finishes come from accumulation, not single shots.
- Question mark: tends to fight tentatively against power punchers (Ngannou loss — never let his hands go).

### Cardio
- Genuinely good five-round gas tank — went 25 minutes vs Ngannou and twice vs Volkov without fading. This is a real edge in championship fights.
