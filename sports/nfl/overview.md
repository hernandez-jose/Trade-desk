# NFL Betting — 2026 Season Overview

## Current Status (April 2026)
The NFL is in the **off-season / draft window**. The 2026 NFL Draft occurs late April.  
Use this period to:
- Evaluate roster construction changes that will affect lines in September
- Track significant free agency moves and their spread/total impact
- Note coaching changes — new coordinators shift a team's offensive or defensive identity

## 2026 Pre-Season ATS Thinking

### Power Rating Adjustments to Track
Before each season, build power ratings from these inputs:

1. **Returning starters** — offensive line and secondary continuity are most predictive
2. **Quarterback** — a new QB is the single biggest variable in a power rating shift
3. **Coaching staff stability** — first-year coordinators typically underperform expectations
4. **Schedule strength** — early-season scheduling advantages create ATS edges

## Key NFL Betting Angles

### Home-Field Advantage
Standard NFL home-field adjustment: **2.5–3 points**  
Exceptions:
- Domed home teams (advantage in dome, disadvantage at cold outdoor venues)
- Newly relocated franchises (home field not yet established)
- Teams with historically loud stadiums (KC, Seattle, SF) get an extra 0.5 point

### Divisional Games
Division games are historically closer than the spread suggests:
- Familiarity reduces the talent gap
- Coaches have more film on division opponents
- Dogs cover at roughly 52–53% in division games historically
- The total is also typically lower — defenses know the opponent's tendencies

### Thursday Night Football (TNF)
Short week bets:
- Road teams on short rest are at a significant disadvantage
- If a team played Sunday and is on the road Thursday — lean against them ATS
- The total is often undervalued — both teams' offenses struggle on short rest → lean under

### Back-to-Back Road Games
Teams playing their second consecutive road game:
- Morale and energy deplete
- Home underdog in this spot is a legitimate angle
- Check: is the road team also on a letdown after a big win?

## Key Numbers to Track

| Key Number | Frequency (% of game margins) |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| 3          | ~15% of all games             |
| 7          | ~9%                           |
| 6          | ~6%                           |
| 10         | ~5%                           |
| 14         | ~5%                           |

**Always** protect yourself around 3 and 7. Buying a half-point through these numbers is almost always worth the extra juice.

## Totals Approach

The NFL total market has become more efficient, but edges still exist:

- **Outdoor cold/wind games**: Weather (see `guides/advanced-strategies.md`) — bet the under early before lines adjust
- **Dome-vs-dome**: Often the over — pace doesn't slow, no weather factor
- **Two top-10 defenses**: Lean under, especially on the road total
- **First-week totals**: Books use last year's data heavily; exploit major roster overhauls

## Playoff Betting Notes

NFL playoffs are the sharpest market of the year. Edges:
- **Home field** is undervalued for top seeds in early rounds
- Avoid big favorites in divisional round — historically covers only ~48%
- Super Bowl prop markets are the least efficient — sharp edges available in exotic props
