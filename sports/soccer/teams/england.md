## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-2-3-1 under Tuchel — high press, aggressive transitions

**How they attack:**
England attack with pace and directness under Tuchel. Bellingham drives from deep — arriving late into the box — while Kane's positioning and off-ball movement creates the central goal threat. Rashford provides width and pace on the left; Foden's technical play on the right creates chances. Set pieces via Bellingham/Foden delivery.

**How they defend:**
High press under Tuchel — England are much more aggressive defensively than in Southgate's era. The back four is solid; Jordan Pickford is reliable. Main vulnerability: pace in behind the high defensive line.

**Pressing Intensity:** High  
**Possession Style:** Dominant — technical and direct mix  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** High — Kane aerial + Bellingham delivery  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Low-Medium
