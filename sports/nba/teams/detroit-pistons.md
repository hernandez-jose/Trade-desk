## 2025–26 Season Overview

The Pistons were the clear-cut best team in the East during the regular season with a 60–22 record — a massive turnaround built around the development of Cade Cunningham into a legitimate franchise superstar. They earned the 1-seed heading into the playoffs and were expected to be heavy favorites to reach the Eastern Conference Finals.

**Offensive identity**: Cunningham-driven, read-and-react offense. Pick-and-roll heavy with Duren as the roll man. Duncan Robinson and Huerter space the floor for Cunningham to operate.

**Defensive identity**: Athletic, switchable defense led by Ausar Thompson. Duren protects the rim.
