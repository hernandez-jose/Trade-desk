## Fighting Style

**Pressure boxer-grappler with elite, fight-ending power for the weight.**

Topuria is a perfectly balanced offensive fighter: a sharp, heavy-handed boxer who walks opponents down behind a tight guard, then detonates a right hand or left hook once they plant their feet. He carries a black belt in BJJ (8 career sub wins) so the grappling is a genuine secondary threat, not a fallback. He fights at a controlled, high-IQ pace and rarely gets into chaotic exchanges — he makes opponents fight in the pocket where his timing and power win.

### Grappling
- 94% takedown defense; very hard to take down and keep down.
- Real submission threat off scrambles and from top — 8 career subs.
- Comfortable closing distance into the clinch and controlling.

### Striking
- Best pure boxing in the lightweight division; compact combinations, excellent counter timing.
- One-shot KO power — stopped Volkanovski and (first ever) Holloway with strikes.
- Good head movement and a high guard; SApM is moderate but defense is solid (64%).

### Cardio
- Has not been forced into deep waters often (most fights end early), but showed composure into R3 vs. Holloway. 5-round cardio at LW is the one partial unknown.
