# Michael Chandler — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Freedom 250 | vs. Mauricio Ruffy | June 14, 2026 | 155 lbs | 3 Rounds

> Analyst note: A 40-year-old, three-fight-skid veteran on a ~14-month layoff stepping in with a fresher, taller, more accurate power striker. Chandler's only realistic edge is his D1 wrestling — but he has spent recent years brawling instead of wrestling, which is exactly the fight Ruffy wants. This is a fade on the moneyline; the only live Chandler bet is a small early-KO longshot prop.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**Case FOR:** Chandler is a former NCAA D1 All-American (Missouri, 5th in 2009) with genuine one-punch power and 13 career first-round finishes. Ruffy's lone UFC loss came on the mat — Saint Denis took him down inside the first minute and finished him in Round 2 — so a blueprint exists. If Chandler commits to wrestling and drags Ruffy into grappling exchanges, he can steal rounds or find a finish, and his power means any clean exchange can end it.
**Case AGAINST:** Chandler is 40, on a three-fight losing streak (1–5 in his last six), coming off a brutal third-round TKO by Pimblett, and hasn't competed since April 2025 (~14-month layoff). His striking defense is poor (SApM 4.58) and he has been finished/out-pointed by the division's better men. Critically, he has increasingly chosen to brawl rather than wrestle — the worst possible game-plan against a 75"-reach, 58%-accuracy power striker who beats people up at range. The most likely path is Ruffy controlling distance and finishing a declining, hittable Chandler.
**Most likely path:** Ruffy by KO/TKO in Rounds 1–2 as Chandler stands and trades.

## 3. Opponent Context
Ruffy is everything that has troubled Chandler lately: a long (75" reach), accurate (58%), heavy-handed striker who controls distance and absorbs little. Ruffy fights tall and unorthodox (spinning wheel kicks, wide stance), which punishes the forward-pressing brawling Chandler now defaults to. Ruffy's one vulnerability — defensive wrestling against a chain-wrestling grappler — is theoretically Chandler's lane, but only if Chandler abandons the brawl and wrestles with discipline he hasn't shown recently. ## 4. Striking Analysis
### Offense
Heavy overhand right, explosive blitzes, real one-punch power. SLpM 4.89 reflects high aggression. His best chance in any exchange is to land first and end it.
### Defense
The glaring weakness. SApM 4.58 (absorbs almost as much as he lands); striking defense roughly mid-50s%. He has been TKO'd by Pimblett and Oliveira. At 40 with eroded reflexes, his ability to avoid Ruffy's accurate, diverse offense is a serious concern.
### Historical Striking Data
SLpM 4.89, accuracy 46%, SApM 4.58, defense ~54% (per ESPN/CBS via search). High-volume, high-risk profile. ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/ufc/fighter/50037713/michael-chandler/))

## 5. Grappling Analysis
### Offensive Wrestling
This is his edge and the only realistic winning path. D1 All-American base; explosive double-legs, slams, strong top control and ground-and-pound. TD avg 2.17 in the UFC, but usage has dropped as he's chosen to brawl.
### Defensive Wrestling
Adequate; TD defense 71%. Not the concern — Ruffy won't be shooting on him.
### Scrambles & Submissions
Low offensive submission threat. Has been submitted (Poirier RNC R3) when scrambling recklessly — back exposure is a risk if he chases.
### Cage Wrestling
Capable of pinning opponents and grinding when committed; the question is whether he commits for 15 minutes rather than seeking the knockout.

## 10. Historical Context
### Style Matchup History
Chandler vs. rangy, accurate strikers has gone poorly when he brawls (Oliveira, Gaethje, Pimblett). His best UFC wins (Hooker, Ferguson) came via early power, not sustained wrestling.
### Comparable Opponent Analysis
Ruffy is a fresher, longer version of the strikers who have beaten Chandler. The Saint Denis fight shows how to beat Ruffy (wrestle early) — but Chandler is not a wrestle-first fighter anymore.
### Career Arc
Clear late-career decline: explosive prime in Bellator/early UFC, now a gatekeeper/name being used to build a young contender on a showcase card.

## 11. Key Paths to Victory
### Path 1: Early power KO (Medium-Low)
Chandler lands the overhand right in a Round 1 exchange and ends it. His single most live path — he has the power and Ruffy will trade.
### Path 2: Takedown-and-grind / GnP finish (Low-Medium)
Chandler abandons the brawl, chains takedowns, exploits Ruffy's defensive-wrestling hole (the Saint Denis blueprint), and controls/finishes on top. Requires discipline he rarely shows.
### Path 3: Gritty decision via wrestling (Low)
Chandler wrestles for 15 minutes and grinds out the cards. Out of character recently and physically demanding at 40.

## 12. X-Factors
- **Age + layoff:** 40 years old, ~14 months out — ring rust and eroded reflexes against a sharp, active opponent.
- **Game-plan discipline:** Will he wrestle or brawl? The whole fight hinges on this and recent history says brawl.
- **White House stage:** Massive occasion; could inspire a career performance or amplify nerves/rust.
- **Power as the equalizer:** In 4-oz gloves, one Chandler right hand keeps him live for ~10 minutes.

## 13. Risk Factors & Confidence Assessment
### Primary Risks to Chandler Winning
Declining chin and high SApM vs. an accurate, rangy power striker; failure to commit to wrestling; age and ring rust; being finished early as he presses forward.
### Confidence Level
Model estimate for Chandler: **~16% to win** (essentially the inverse of Ruffy's ~84% cap-adjusted figure). This is a fade. His non-trivial KO power keeps him from being a true no-chance dog, which is why the early-KO prop — not the moneyline — is the only Chandler-side consideration. At +390/+500 the implied probability (~17–20%) roughly matches or slightly exceeds the model, so there is **no moneyline value** on Chandler.
