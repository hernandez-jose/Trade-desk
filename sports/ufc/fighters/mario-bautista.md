## Style Profile
- **Primary:** Submission wrestling with credible boxing
- **Striking:** Pressure-based combinations, switches stances fluidly. High volume (5.33 SLpM). Prefers to establish aggression and trade at mid-range before clinching or getting to the mat.
- **Grappling:** Elite submission hunter — 4 UFC submission victories, tied for third-most in UFC bantamweight history. Rear-naked choke, guillotine, and body triangle specialist. Averages 1.92 takedowns per 15 minutes.
- **Defense:** 66% TD defense suggests good takedown awareness. 54% striking defense is below average for the top-10 in the division — he accepts volume to deliver volume.
- **Weaknesses:** Has been dominated by elite wrestlers (Umar Nurmagomedov); absorbed with 3.91 SApM, meaning he takes damage; struggled against high-level submission specialists in early career (Sandhagen armbar 2019).
