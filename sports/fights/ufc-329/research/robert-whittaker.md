## Style Notes for This Matchup

- **Reach deficit:** Whittaker gives up 4 inches in reach to Krylov (73.5 vs 77.5 in) — must work inside to neutralize Krylov's long-range weapons
- **Striking output vs. Krylov's defense:** Whittaker's ~4.50 SLpM volume approach will test Krylov's 52% strike defense — historically Krylov has absorbed clean shots
- **Takedown defense vs. Krylov grappling:** Whittaker's 82% TD defense (career) is excellent; Krylov's submission threat is real but Whittaker has historically defended well off his back
- **Size/physicality at 205:** Whittaker is the smaller man; Krylov will be the naturally larger fighter — question is whether Whittaker's extra mass from not cutting will compensate
- **Chin question:** Whittaker has been stopped by Du Plessis (2023), submitted by Chimaev (2024); at 35 and moving up, there are legitimate questions about his durability to heavier shots
- **Combination striking:** Whittaker's fast, sharp combos out of orthodox stance may be well-suited to catching Krylov on the way in — Krylov walks forward and gets hit coming in (Reyes, Guskov both KO'd him standing)

## Situational Factors

- **Weight class debut:** First career fight at LHW — unknown how he performs at the new weight; could be refreshed (no cut) or could be undersized
- **Momentum:** Back-to-back losses entering this fight (Chimaev R1 sub, de Ridder SD) — worst two-fight skid of his career; considerable pressure to perform
- **Age:** 35 years old at fight time; entering the back-nine of his prime; city-kickboxing gym environments tend to keep fighters sharp long
- **Motivation:** Moving to 205 is framed as a new chapter — potential upside in urgency and freshness vs. downside of unknown factors at new weight
- **Schedule spot:** UFC 329 is International Fight Week (Las Vegas, July 11) — high-profile card with McGregor vs. Holloway headlining; Whittaker performs well on big stages historically
- **No advantage from weight cut elimination:** Both fighters will have had equivalent prep time (fight announced May 2026 for July 2026)
