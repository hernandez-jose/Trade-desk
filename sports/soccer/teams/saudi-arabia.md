## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-2-3-1 or 5-3-2 depending on opponent quality

**How they attack:**
Saudi Arabia's most dangerous weapon is quick transitions and counter-attacks. Salem Al-Dawsari drives forward from the left and cuts inside. They exploit space behind high defensive lines using Al-Buraikan as a target. Under Mancini, they've become more organized but remain a counter-attack first team.

**How they defend:**
Compact mid-block or low-block depending on the opponent's quality. They defend in two banks of four, pressing selectively when the opponent turns the ball over. Historical vulnerability: set pieces and aerial balls into the box.

**Pressing Intensity:** Low-Medium  
**Possession Style:** Counter-attack  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium-High
