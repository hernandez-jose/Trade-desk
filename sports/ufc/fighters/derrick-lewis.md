## Fighting Style

**One-punch knockout artist — the most prolific KO finisher in UFC history, with a low-volume, all-or-nothing game.**

Lewis is pure, terrifying power. He throws low volume (2.43 SLpM) and waits for one clean shot — and at heavyweight one shot ends nights, which is how he became the all-time UFC KO leader (16). But the package around the power has eroded: cardio is famously poor (he visibly gasses, often after one round), takedown defense is below average (53%), and at 41 his speed and recovery have slipped. His losses follow a clear script — get taken down or out-paced, then stopped or out-pointed. He is dangerous for as long as the fight lasts in the first round and increasingly vulnerable after.

### Grappling
- Minimal offensive wrestling (0.53 TD/15, 26% acc). 53% TDD is exploitable; strong wrestlers have controlled and finished him (Almeida, Cortes-Acosta off a takedown sequence).
- Not a submission threat; ground game is survival, not offense.

### Striking
- Elite one-shot power, especially the overhand right and uppercut. 50% accuracy — efficient.
- Low output and 40% defense; he is not a volume technician and can be outworked.

### Cardio
- Career-long weakness. Gasses early; his danger window is essentially Round 1 (and dwindling). Lost a 5-round decision to Almeida; faded/stopped R2 vs. Cortes-Acosta.
