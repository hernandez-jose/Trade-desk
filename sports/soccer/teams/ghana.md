## Tactical Profile

**Default System:** 4-3-3 / 4-2-3-1 with Kudus as the creative threat

**How they attack:**
Ghana attacks through Mohammed Kudus' creativity and dribbling. They are physical and direct, with Jordan Ayew providing the focal point. Pace on the wings and physical play in midfield. Set pieces are a source — physical players in the box.

**How they defend:**
Mid-block defensive structure. Thomas Partey provides the anchor. Ghana can be compact and difficult to break down when organized. Vulnerable to high-tempo combinations from elite teams.

**Pressing Intensity:** Medium  
**Possession Style:** Direct — physical play  
**Set-Piece Threat (offense):** Medium  
**Set-Piece Vulnerability (defense):** Medium
