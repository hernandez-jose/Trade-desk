## Fighting Style

**High-output, aggressive young striker who takes early risks.**

Aswell is a 25-year-old Texan striker who blends volume boxing with a willingness to open up and hunt finishes early. He fights forward and throws in bunches. The book on him so far: explosive and dangerous in the first round, but beatable over three rounds by more polished, well-rounded opponents who weather the early storm and out-work him later (Oki, Riley both did exactly that).

### Grappling
- Low takedown volume (0.32/15min); he is not a wrestler and prefers to keep it standing. Ground game largely untested at UFC level.

### Striking
- Primary weapon: high-volume, aggressive boxing-led striking. Real R1 power (multiple early finishes regionally + Almeida).

### Cardio
- Question mark — both UFC losses were decisions where he faded relative to the opponent's output. May invest too much early.
