## Fighting Style

**Elite kickboxer / counter-power striker with fight-ending one-shot power**

Pereira is a former GLORY two-division kickboxing champion who translates that pedigree directly into MMA. He fights at range, controls distance with a long, heavy jab and a calf kick, then detonates a left hook and a left-hand/right-hook counter. He is patient, lands in low volume but with extreme accuracy and concussive power. He wins by hurting people, not by accumulating points.

### Grappling
- Almost no offensive wrestling; submission game is non-existent (0 career subs).
- TD defense (70%) is functional but has never been stress-tested by an elite heavyweight wrestler/clinch player.
- Vulnerable on the mat if controlled — survived being grounded vs Błachowicz and in the Ankalaev I loss.

### Striking
- World-class calf kick — has crippled opponents' mobility (Rountree, Adesanya II).
- One-punch KO power in both hands; arguably the hardest hitter at 205, now at HW.
- Excellent timing and counters; thrives against opponents who come to him.

### Cardio
- Conditioning was a question early (faded late vs Adesanya I when he still won, struggled in Ankalaev I decision loss); has improved but 5-round volume against a mover is a concern, now carrying HW mass.
