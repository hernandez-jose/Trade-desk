## Fighting Style

**Long-range, high-precision counter-striker with one-shot finishing power.**

O'Malley is one of the most accurate and creative strikers in bantamweight history. He fights at the end of his 72" reach, controls distance with a snapping jab and front/teep kicks, and lands fight-ending power shots — particularly a straight right and a check-hook counter — when opponents step in. His game is built to keep the fight standing and on his terms; the central betting question in any O'Malley fight is whether the opponent can take him down and keep him there.

### Grappling
- Offensive wrestling is negligible — he is not a takedown threat.
- Takedown defense is the swing variable: serviceable vs. non-wrestlers, but Merab Dvalishvili dominated him on the mat in both fights (decision, then a R3 north-south choke at UFC 316).
- Limited submission offense; his danger off his back is survival/scramble-based, not offensive.

### Striking
- Elite accuracy (61%) and volume (6.70 SLpM) at range.
- Length + footwork + timing; lethal counters when opponents close distance.
- Mixes levels with body kicks and jabs to set up the right hand.

### Cardio
- Showed he can go 25 hard minutes (Vera UFC 299, Merab I UFC 306) without gassing.
- Pace is more measured/sniping than relentless pressure; conserves output for clean shots.
