# Michael Aswell Jr. — Deep Fight Research
## UFC Fight Night: Kape vs. Horiguchi (UFC Vegas 119) | vs. Gaston Bolaños | June 20, 2026 | 145 lbs | 3 Rounds (Prelims)

> Analyst note: Aswell is the betting favorite (~-380/-400) on youth, aggression, and high output — but the price massively overstates the gap. He is 1-2 in the UFC, both losses are decision losses where he was out-worked over 15 minutes, and his one win came against a broken-handed opponent. Against a defensively sound counter-striker who he cannot take down, the favorite tag looks like a public/recency overlay, not a skill chasm.
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The case FOR Aswell: 25 years old, fast, aggressive, high-volume striker (SLpM 7.79) with genuine R1 finishing power and a 3-month-fresh turnaround. He'll come forward and can end it early if he catches the older, rusty Bolaños. The case AGAINST: he is 1-2 in the UFC, both losses are unanimous decisions where more polished opponents (Oki, Riley) weathered his early flurry and out-pointed him; his lone UFC win (Almeida, R1 TKO) came against an opponent with a visibly broken hand; he has no wrestling to fall back on (0.32 TD/15min); and he tends to fade after investing heavily early. Most likely path: an early KO/TKO in R1-2 if he lands clean; otherwise he is at real risk of being out-classed at range and losing another decision.

*(Sources: UFC.com, UFCStats, Tapology, ESPN, mmadecisions.com)*

## 3. Opponent Context
Bolaños is a veteran Muay Thai counter-striker with strong defensive metrics (SApM 2.57, 52% striking def) and zero takedown threat to worry about, but a poor takedown defense (42%) and a chin that's been cracked (McGhee TKO). For Aswell, the threat is being out-techniqued and countered at range by the more varied striker, and being unable to use any grappling Plan B. The opportunity is Bolaños's age (33), ~13-month layoff, and crackable chin: if Aswell's R1 power lands, this ends fast.
