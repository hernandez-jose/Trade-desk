## Style Profile
- **Primary Style:** Elite striker with high accuracy and volume; well-rounded with active grappling
- **Striking:** Wushu (Sanda) and amateur boxing foundation; 2013 World Wushu Championships gold medalist; 2014 Asian Games gold medalist; 2019 World Amateur Boxing Championships silver medalist — unparalleled striking credentials for the division
- **Grappling:** 75% takedown accuracy with 1.61 TD/15 min; 100% takedown defense (career); submission average of 1.1/15 min is surprisingly active
- **Cardio:** Efficient striker with positive differential (+4.03 SLpM minus SApM); fights smart, doesn't carry unnecessary damage
- **Weaknesses:** Only career loss is by submission (RNC vs. Fernandes, Nov 2024) after being dropped — ground-and-pound vulnerability identified; limited MMA sample size (only 10 pro fights)
