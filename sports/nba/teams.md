# NBA Team Betting Profiles — 2025–26 Season

> Update after each week of play with ATS records and notable trends.
> Format: Team | Pace Rank | Def Rating | Key Betting Notes

---

## Eastern Conference

### Boston Celtics
- **Identity**: Defensive-minded with elite shooting; historically undervalued as road underdogs
- **Totals lean**: Under in tight playoff matchups; over in blowout situations where they lock in
- **ATS note**: Historically covers as home favorites when healthy; fades as road chalk at -6+

### Cleveland Cavaliers
- **Identity**: System-dependent; strong when healthy, vulnerable when stars sit
- **Totals lean**: Track pace — they play at one of the lower tempos in the East
- **ATS note**: Back as home dogs; fade as road favorites

### New York Knicks
- **Identity**: Physical, defensive, crowd-driven home team. Madison Square Garden is one of the best home courts.
- **Totals lean**: Under in playoff games; physical style grinds games down
- **ATS note**: Historically strong as home underdogs at MSG

### Milwaukee Bucks
- **Identity**: Giannis-centric offense — monitor his health status closely
- **Totals lean**: When Giannis is active and healthy, lean over in neutral venue/dome games
- **ATS note**: Fade as road favorite without a full week of rest

### Indiana Pacers
- **Identity**: Fastest pace in the East; pushes totals way up
- **Totals lean**: Strong over lean; when playing another fast team, add 5–8 to the posted total
- **ATS note**: Volatile ATS; best as home underdogs in a shootout

### Miami Heat
- **Identity**: Culture-above-all; known for overperforming in the playoffs (Erik Spoelstra effect)
- **Totals lean**: Lower-scoring games under Spoelstra; under lean in playoff spots
- **ATS note**: **Back Miami as playoff underdogs** — historically one of the best playoff dog teams

### Philadelphia 76ers
- **Identity**: Injury-plagued historically; track health weekly
- **Totals lean**: When both Embiid and secondary stars are active, lean over
- **ATS note**: Monitor star availability before every bet

### Atlanta Hawks
- **Identity**: Fast pace, inconsistent defense
- **Totals lean**: Over in home games; fast pace inflates totals
- **ATS note**: Fade as road favorites; back as large home dogs

## Western Conference

### Oklahoma City Thunder
- **Identity**: Young, athletic, deep roster. Strong defensive system.
- **Totals lean**: Under in playoff games (defensive identity)
- **ATS note**: Strong home ATS; monitor if stars play heavy playoff minutes without rest

### Denver Nuggets
- **Identity**: Half-court offense; altitude home advantage; Jokic-driven system
- **Totals lean**: Under in home games (altitude affects pace); over in neutral venues
- **ATS note**: **Back at home always** — altitude + Jokic system = most reliable home-court edge in the NBA

### Minnesota Timberwolves
- **Identity**: Elite defense; systematic offensive execution
- **Totals lean**: Hard under lean; they hold opponents 5+ below average
- **ATS note**: Back as home dogs against offensive powerhouses

### Golden State Warriors
- **Identity**: Motion-based offense; historically great but aging core
- **Totals lean**: Over when young legs are playing heavy minutes; under in high-pressure spots
- **ATS note**: Monitor aging rotation; back in early rounds as dogs if healthy

### Los Angeles Lakers
- **Identity**: Most-bet team in the NBA (public favorite); consistently overpriced
- **Totals lean**: Neutral — depends entirely on LeBron and star availability
- **ATS note**: **Fade as road favorites** — one of the most reliable fades in recent NBA history

### Phoenix Suns
- **Identity**: Star-driven; inconsistent when supporting cast underperforms
- **Totals lean**: Over when all stars are active and healthy
- **ATS note**: Volatile; avoid unless strong situational angle exists

### Dallas Mavericks
- **Identity**: Luka-dependent; explosive offense, inconsistent defense
- **Totals lean**: Over in home games; Luka inflates scoring output
- **ATS note**: Avoid as large road favorites; back as home dogs

### Memphis Grizzlies
- **Identity**: Physical, fast, deep bench
- **Totals lean**: Over in home games (fast pace + physical play = fouls + FTs)
- **ATS note**: Strong home ATS; back when healthy

### Sacramento Kings
- **Identity**: High-pace offense; inconsistent defense
- **Totals lean**: Over lean — they play fast and give up points
- **ATS note**: Back at home as underdogs; fade on the road vs. elite defenses
