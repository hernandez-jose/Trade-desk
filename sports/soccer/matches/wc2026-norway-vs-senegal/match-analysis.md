## Key Tactical Battles

### Battle 1 — Haaland vs. Senegal's CB Partnership
Senegal's center backs are physical and experienced in African football but have not faced a striker of Haaland's combination of pace, physicality, and technical quality at the WC level. Haaland's movement is elite — he will make runs in behind and also hold up play. **Advantage: Norway/Haaland.**

### Battle 2 — Mané vs. Norwegian Fullbacks
Senegal's best path to an upset is Mané isolating Norwegian fullbacks. Norwegian fullbacks are competent but not elite defenders in 1v1. If Mané gets in behind, Senegal have a goal threat. **Advantage: Senegal on this specific battle.**

### Battle 3 — Ødegaard vs. Senegal's Midfield Press
Ødegaard's ability to escape pressure and create from the #10 role is the key to Norway's chance creation. Senegal's Gana Gueye provides a physical screen but Ødegaard is clever enough to find pockets. **Slight Norway advantage.**
