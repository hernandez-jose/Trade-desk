## 1. Executive Summary
The case FOR Rosa: she is the more proven UFC commodity (8–4 UFC, No. 8 ranked), the highest-volume striker in the matchup (6.49 SLpM, 56% accuracy), durable, and has solid 68% takedown defense to keep this standing where she banks rounds. The case AGAINST: she absorbs a lot (4.91 SApM, only 49% striking defense), her recent wins are grind-it-out decisions, and Santos is a younger, fresher wrestle-grappler who can erase Rosa's volume with takedowns and control time. Most likely path for Rosa: keep it standing, out-volume Santos over three rounds for a decision. The risk is Santos turning it into a grappling match.

## 3. Opponent Context
Luana Santos (10–2, 5–1 UFC) is a grappling-first fighter with 86% takedown defense, 2.17 TD/15 offense, and a 1.21 sub/15 average (RNC and keylock finishes). She specifically threatens Rosa's weakest area: Rosa's striking defense is only 49% and she gives up nearly 5 sig strikes/min, but the bigger danger is being taken down and controlled. Santos's low striking volume (2.99 SLpM) means if Rosa keeps it standing she should win the exchanges; if Santos gets it down, Rosa's rounds evaporate.
