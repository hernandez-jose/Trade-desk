## Wrigley Field — The Most Important Betting Variable

Wrigley Field is unique in all of MLB. The wind is the single most important factor when betting any Cubs home game.

| Wind Direction       | Effect on Totals            | Betting Lean     |
|---------------------|-----------------------------|------------------|
| Blowing out (E→W)   | +2 to +3 runs               | Strong OVER      |
| Blowing in (W→E)    | −2 to −3 runs               | Strong UNDER     |
| Cross wind          | Minimal total impact        | Neutral          |
| Calm (< 5 mph)      | Slight pitcher's advantage  | Mild UNDER lean  |

**Rule**: Never bet a Cubs home game total without checking the wind forecast on Weather.com or Wunderground for Wrigleyville. This is one of the most exploitable edges in MLB betting.

## Betting Angles

### Home Moneyline
- Cubs are frequently **overpriced at home** due to Wrigley nostalgia and market sentiment
- **Lean**: Fade Cubs as home favorites above -145 unless starting a true ace
- **Lean**: Back Cubs as home underdogs (+120 or better) with wind blowing out

### Run Line
- **Cubs -1.5**: Only if ace is starting AND wind is blowing out (run-heavy environment)
- **Cubs +1.5**: High value when getting reasonable price with wind blowing in (low-scoring game)

### Totals
- **This is where the edge lives with the Cubs**
- Check wind direction before any total bet — this overrides all other analysis
- Under lean when wind blows in regardless of matchup
- Over lean when wind blows out regardless of pitcher quality

### Road Games
- Cubs road record historically volatile — inconsistent travel performance
- **April road edge**: West Coast teams hosting Cubs in afternoon games (time zone fatigue)
