## Background & Credentials

- **High school wrestling state champion** — Ontario High School, Oregon. Started 0-win freshman year, built to state title by senior year — a documented comeback story
- **Amateur boxing record: 8-1** — genuine striking pedigree before turning professional
- Briefly attended Boise State University (sports psychology) before dropping out due to family matters
- **Former Front Street Fights Featherweight Champion** — Boise, Idaho regional circuit
- **UFL Bantamweight Champion** — won title via Peruvian Necktie submission (R3) over Hunter Azure, August 30, 2024
- UFC stint #1: Debuted 2018; was released after losing record
- UFC stint #2: Re-signed after UFL title win; returned to UFC in 2024
- Three consecutive UFC decision losses heading into Perth fight (Lapilus, Smith, Rosas Jr.)

## Fighting Style

**Explosive finisher with a high-level grappling toolbox**

Morales is defined by his finishing instinct — 75% of 16 wins end before the scorecards. But his UFC record (3-8) tells the story of what happens when elite wrestlers and technical fighters neutralize his offense.

### Striking
- Amateur boxing background (8-1) provides a legitimate stand-up foundation
- 7 career KO/TKO wins — genuine knockout power at 135 lbs
- Front Street Fights featherweight champion — tested his striking at regional level
- Tends to be aggressive, pressure-forward; can leave openings when reaching for knockouts

### Grappling
- Five different submission types used across his career — exceptional grappling variety
- Peruvian Necktie is his signature finishing hold — rare and dangerous from front headlock
- Brabo choke, Anaconda — demonstrates deep understanding of no-gi choke entries
- Wrestling base from high school: takedowns are part of his game but not elite-level

### Cardio
- 35 years old — three consecutive 3-round decision losses suggest either cardio decline or game-plan issues at UFC pace
- His finishes tend to come early (KO in R1 vs Smolka; submissions before R3 typically)
- If fights go past Round 2 without a finish, his output appears to decline
