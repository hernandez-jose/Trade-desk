## Fighting Style

**Explosive wrestle-boxer / athlete-brawler — former D1 All-American wrestler and Fresno State fullback who pressures with power and pace.**

Hokit is an elite-athlete heavyweight: an All-American wrestler (peaked #3 in the nation) and college football fullback/TE who fights aggressively, comes forward, and lands in volume. His base is wrestling (TDD 75%, TD 3.89/15) but he wins fights with hands — heavy, accurate power early. The trade-off is a porous defense (44%, 6.81 SApM): he wins firefights but eats shots doing it, which is the live risk vs. a one-shot KO artist. Still raw at elite level — only one UFC fight (Blaydes) tested him past Round 1.

### Grappling
- D1 All-American wrestler; strong takedowns and 75% TDD. Can dictate where the fight goes against non-wrestlers.
- 3 career submissions — opportunistic on the mat, not a pure submission hunter.

### Striking
- Heavy, accurate power, especially in the first round (both UFC TKOs came in R1).
- Defensively raw: 44% defense, high absorption rate — trades and can be hit clean.

### Cardio
- Only one fight past Round 1 at UFC level (3-rd war with Blaydes — went the distance), so 15-minute gas tank is shown but not deeply tested. Athletic base suggests good conditioning.
