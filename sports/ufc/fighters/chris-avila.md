## Fighting Style

**Brawler with Cesar Gracie BJJ foundation — aggressive finisher who loses all his decisions**

Avila's career splits cleanly in two directions: when he wins, he finishes. When he loses, he gets outpointed. His 8 wins contain 7 finishes (87.5% finish rate); all 9 losses are by decision.

### Striking
- Trained heavily in boxing since 2021 — his hands are arguably the sharpest they have ever been
- 30% accuracy suggests wild output rather than technical precision
- 5 KO/TKO wins show genuine finishing power when he connects
- Philly Shell and boxing fundamentals are his primary stand-up defense tool
- **No kick game** — almost entirely hands-based, consistent with his boxing background

### Grappling
- Trains under the Cesar Gracie / Nate Diaz camp — legitimate BJJ infrastructure
- 2 submission wins in career confirm he can threaten from the ground
- **46% takedown defense** is below average — can be taken down; not an elite grappler defensively
- Grappling is his secondary weapon but has been untouched for ~5 years of boxing

### Durability
- **Has never been stopped in his professional career** — this is his single most important stat for betting
- All 9 losses came by decision, meaning he survived every exchange but lost the scorecards
- Durable chin; does not fold under pressure

### Ring Rust (Critical Factor)
- Last MMA fight: **~2021 — approximately 5 years before this bout**
- Has been competing exclusively in boxing during this period
- MMA-specific skills (clinch work, takedown defense, cage control, transition game) are likely severely degraded
- Boxing footwork and boxing defense do not directly transfer to MMA range management
