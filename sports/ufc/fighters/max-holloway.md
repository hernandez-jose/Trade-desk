## Style Profile
- **Primary Style:** High-volume pressure striker, elite cardio-based volume boxer
- **Strengths:**
  - Volume output — 7.17 SLpM is elite level; accumulates damage over full fight
  - UFC all-time leader in significant strikes landed (3,655+) and total strikes (3,907+)
  - Elite cardio — historically strongest in championship rounds (R4–R5)
  - High takedown defense (84%) — neutralizes grappling threats effectively
  - Versatile striking — head, body, and leg kicks; never stops throwing
  - Can fight at distance or in a phone booth — adapts to range well
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Reach disadvantage (69") — shorter than most LW and WW opponents
  - Has been KO'd clean — Topuria R3 (2024) showed elite chin can be cracked
  - Decision losses suggest world-class opposition can manage distance and volume
  - Moving up to WW (170) for first time — unknown weight class variables
  - Recent form: 1-2 in last three fights heading into UFC 329
