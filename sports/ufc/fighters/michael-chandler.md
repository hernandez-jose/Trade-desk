## Fighting Style

**Explosive wrestle-boxer / pressure brawler with elite first-round danger.**

Chandler is a former NCAA Division I All-American wrestler (Missouri, 5th in 2009) who built a Bellator title run on takedowns plus heavy hands. In the UFC he has increasingly chosen to brawl rather than wrestle — leaning on power, blitzes, and grit. That choice produced highlight wins (Hooker, Ferguson) but also got him hurt and out-grappled against the division's best. His engine is built for short, violent bursts; he fades and becomes hittable in deeper waters.

### Grappling
- Elite offensive wrestling pedigree; explosive double-legs and slams when he commits.
- Strong top control and ground-and-pound when on top.
- Has been submitted (Poirier RNC) when chasing and getting reckless; back exposure is a risk.

### Striking
- Heavy overhand right, fight-ending power, aggressive blitzes.
- Defensively porous — high SApM, recent finishes absorbed (Pimblett, Oliveira).
- Pace can be wild; sacrifices position/defense for offense.

### Cardio
- Explosive early; output and defense decline notably after Round 1 in tough fights.
- At 40 with a long layoff, late-round durability is a major question.
